> [!definition]
> The **explanatory variable** $x_i$ is the variable used to explain or predict the [[Dependent Variable]]. It is also called an **independent variable**, **predictor variable**, or **regressor**.

In the simple regression model

$$
y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_i+u_i,
$$

$\beta_1$ describes the change in the predicted value of $y$ associated with a one-unit increase in $x$.

> [!important] Terminology and interpretation
> **Independent variable** does not mean that $x_i$ is statistically independent of the Dependent Variable or of the error term. A **control variable** is usually an additional regressor included in a multiple regression to hold another factor fixed; it is not a general synonym for the single explanatory variable in simple regression. Calling $x$ an explanatory variable does not by itself establish a causal effect.

For example, when a model uses return on equity to explain CEO salary, return on equity is the explanatory variable and salary is the Dependent Variable.

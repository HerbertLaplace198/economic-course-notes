The population regression model is:

$$
y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + u_i
$$

- $y_i$: [[Dependent Variable]]
- $x_i$: [[Explanatory Variable]]
- $\beta_0$: population intercept
- $\beta_1$: population slope coefficient
- $u_i$: population error term, representing other influences not explained by the model

> [!note] Meaning of the PRF
> The Population Regression Function describes the true relationship between $x$ and $y$ in the population. However, the population parameters $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ are usually unobservable.

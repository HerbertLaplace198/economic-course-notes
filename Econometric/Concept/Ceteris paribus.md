
> [!definition]
> **Ceteris paribus** means **other things being equal** or **holding all other relevant factors constant**.

It is used to isolate the relationship between one explanatory factor and an outcome while preventing changes in other relevant factors from contaminating the comparison.

## General form

Suppose an economic outcome depends on several variables:

$$
y=f(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_k).
$$

The ceteris paribus effect of $x_j$ on $y$ is the change in $y$ caused by changing $x_j$ while holding the other arguments fixed:

$$
\frac{\partial y}{\partial x_j}
$$

with

$$
x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1},x_{j+1},\ldots,x_k
$$

held constant.

> [!important]
> Ceteris paribus does not claim that other factors literally remain unchanged in the real world. It defines the comparison required to isolate one particular relationship.


$$  
SST=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\bar y)^2  
$$

SST measures the **total variation** in $y$ around its sample mean.
For an OLS regression containing an intercept:

$$  
\boxed{SST=SSE+SSR}  
$$

In words:
$$\text{Total variation}=\text{Explained variation}  + \text{Unexplained variation}  
$$

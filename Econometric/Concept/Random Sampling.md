Suppose the population model is

$$
y_i
=
\beta_0+\beta_1x_{i1}+\cdots+\beta_kx_{ik}+u_i.
$$

> [!definition]
> A **random sample** consists of observations
> $$
> \{(y_i,x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ik}):i=1,\ldots,n\}
> $$
> that are independently and identically distributed draws from the same population.

This is commonly written as

$$
(y_i,x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ik})
\overset{iid}{\sim}
F,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,n,
$$

where $F$ is the population distribution.

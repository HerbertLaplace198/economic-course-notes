> [!definition]
> The **dependent variable** $y_i$ is the outcome that a regression model seeks to explain or predict. It is also called the **explained variable**, **response variable**, or **regressand**.

In the simple regression model

$$
y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_i+u_i,
$$

$y_i$ is the observed outcome for observation $i$. The fitted value $\hat y_i$ is the model’s prediction of that outcome.

> [!important] Observed outcome vs. fitted value
> $y_i$ and $\hat y_i$ are not interchangeable. The difference $y_i-\hat y_i$ is the [[Sample Regression Function (SRF)|residual]]. The phrase **predicted variable** may refer to the outcome being predicted, but **predicted value** refers specifically to $\hat y_i$.

For example, when a model uses return on equity to explain CEO salary, salary is the dependent variable and return on equity is the [[Explanatory Variable]].

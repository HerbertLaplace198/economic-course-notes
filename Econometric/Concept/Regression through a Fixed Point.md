## Regression through the origin

Choosing $(x_0,y_0)=(0,0)$ gives [[Regression through the Origin and Regression on a Constant|regression through the origin]]:

$$
\tilde\beta_1=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_iy_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i^2},
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\tilde\beta_1\mid\mathbf{x})
=\frac{\sigma^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i^2}.
$$

The sample is used to estimate the population regression function:

$$
y_i = \hat{\beta}_0 + \hat{\beta}_1 x_i + e_i
$$

The corresponding fitted value is:

$$
\hat{y}_i = \hat{\beta}_0 + \hat{\beta}_1 x_i
$$

The Residual is:

$$
\hat{u}_i = e_i = y_i - \hat{y}_i
$$

- $\hat{\beta}_0$: estimated sample intercept
- $\hat{\beta}_1$: estimated sample slope coefficient
- $\hat{y}_i$: fitted value of $y_i$
- $e_i$: sample Residual, equal to the difference between the observed and fitted values

> [!important] Error term vs. Residual
> $u_i$ is the unobservable population error term, whereas $e_i$ is the Residual calculated from the sample and estimated model. They are related but are not identical.

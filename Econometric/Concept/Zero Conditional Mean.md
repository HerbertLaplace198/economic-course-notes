> [!definition]
> The **zero conditional mean assumption** requires
> $$
> E(u_i\mid x_i)=0
> $$
> for every possible value of $x_i$.

In a multiple regression model, the corresponding assumption is

$$
E(u_i\mid x_{i1},x_{i2},\ldots,x_{ik})=0.
$$

Using matrix notation, it is commonly written as

$$
E(u\mid X)=0.
$$

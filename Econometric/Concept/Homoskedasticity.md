
> [!definition]
> **Homoskedasticity** requires the conditional variance of the error term to be constant:
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid x_i)=\sigma^2
> $$
> for every possible value of $x_i$.

This means that the spread of $u_i$ does not change with $x_i$. If the spread changes, the errors are **heteroskedastic**.

Homoskedasticity is used to derive the conventional OLS standard errors. It is not required to compute the OLS estimates.

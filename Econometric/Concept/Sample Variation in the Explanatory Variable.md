Consider the simple linear regression model

$$
y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_i+u_i.
$$

> [!definition]
> The Explanatory Variable must vary across observations:
> $$
> \sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar x)^2>0.
> $$
> Equivalently, not all observations may have the same value of $x$.

This condition is sometimes described as requiring **sample variation in the Explanatory Variable**.

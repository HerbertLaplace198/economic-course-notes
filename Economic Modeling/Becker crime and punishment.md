# CHICAGO JOURNALS 

# Crime and Punishment: An Economic Approach 

Gary S. Becker*<br>Columbia University

## I. Introduction

Since the turn of the century, legislation in Western countries has expanded rapidly to reverse the brief dominance of laissez faire during the nineteenth century. The state no longer merely protects against violations of person and property through murder, rape, or burglary but also restricts "discrimination" against certain minorities, collusive business arrangements, "jaywalking," travel, the materials used in construction, and thousands of other activities. The activities restricted not only are numerous but also range widely, affecting persons in very different pursuits and of diverse social backgrounds, education levels, ages, races, etc. Moreover, the likelihood that an offender will be discovered and convicted and the nature and extent of punishments differ greatly from person to person and activity to activity. Yet, in spite of such diversity, some common properties are shared by practically all legislation, and these properties form the subject matter of this essay.

自世纪之交以来，西方国家的立法迅速扩展，以扭转十九世纪短暂的自由放任主导地位。国家不再仅仅通过谋杀、强奸或入室盗窃来保护个人和财产免受侵害，还限制对某些少数群体的“歧视”、共谋商业安排、“乱穿马路”、旅行、建筑材料的使用以及成千上万的其他活动。这些被限制的活动不仅数量众多，而且范围广泛，影响到从事非常不同职业和具有多样社会背景、教育水平、年龄、种族等的人。此外，违法者被发现和定罪的可能性，以及惩罚的性质和程度因人而异、因活动而异。然而，尽管存在如此多样性，几乎所有立法都有一些共同特征，而这些特征构成了本文的主题. 

In the first place, obedience to law is not taken for granted, and public and private resources are generally spent in order both to prevent offenses and to apprehend offenders. In the second place, conviction is not generally considered sufficient punishment in itself; additional and sometimes severe punishments are meted out to those convicted. What determines the amount and type of resources and punishments used to enforce a piece of legislation? In particular, why does enforcement differ so greatly among different kinds of legislation?

首先，**遵守法律并不被视为理所当然**，公共和私人资源通常被用于预防犯罪和逮捕犯罪者。其次，**定罪通常不被视为足够的惩罚**；对被定罪者还会施加额外且有时是严厉的惩罚。是什么决定了用于**执行一项立法的资源和惩罚的数量和类型**？特别是，为什么不同类型的立法在执行上差异如此之大？

The main purpose of this essay is to answer normative versions of these questions, namely, how many resources and how much punishment should be used to enforce different kinds of legislation? Put equivalently, although more strangely, how many offenses should be permitted and how many offenders should go unpunished? The method used formulates a measure of the social loss from offenses and finds those expenditures of resources and punishments that minimize this loss. The general criterion of social loss is shown to incorporate as special cases, valid under special assumptions, the criteria of vengeance, deterrence, compensation, and rehabilitation that historically have figured so prominently in practice and criminological literature.

这篇文章的主要目的是回答这些问题的规范性版本，**即应该使用多少资源和多少惩罚来执行不同类型的立法**？换句话说，虽然更奇怪的是，**应该允许多少犯罪行为**，**多少罪犯应该不受惩罚**？所使用的方法制定了一种衡量犯罪造成的社会损失的标准，并找出那些能够最小化这种损失的资源和惩罚支出。社会损失的一般标准被证明可以作为特殊情况纳入，在特定假设下有效的复仇、威慑、赔偿和康复标准，这些标准在历史上在实践和犯罪学文献中占据了重要地位. 

The optimal amount of enforcement is shown to depend on, among other things, the cost of catching and convicting offenders, the nature of punishments-for example, whether they are fines or prison terms-and the responses of offenders to changes in enforcement. The discussion, therefore, inevitably enters into issues in penology and theories of criminal behavior. A second, although because of lack of space subsidiary, aim of this essay is to see what insights into these questions are provided by our "economic" approach. It is suggested, for example, that a useful theory of criminal behavior can dispense with special theories of anomie, psychological inadequacies, or inheritance of special traits and simply extend the economist's usual analysis of choice.

最佳的执法量显示出依赖于其他因素，包括抓捕和定罪罪犯的成本、惩罚的性质——例如，它们是罚款还是监禁——以及罪犯对执法变化的反应。因此，讨论不可避免地涉及到刑罚学和犯罪行为理论的问题。这篇文章的第二个目标，尽管由于篇幅限制是附属的，是探讨我们的“经济”方法对这些问题提供了哪些见解。例如，**建议一个有用的犯罪行为理论可以不依赖于特殊的失范理论、心理缺陷或特殊特征的遗传，而是简单地扩展经济学家通常的选择分析**。

# II. Basic Analysis 

## A. The Cost of Crime

Although the word "crime" is used in the title to minimize terminological innovations, the analysis is intended to be sufficiently general to cover all violations, not just felonies-like murder, robbery, and assault, which receive so much newspaper coverage-but also tax evasion, the so-called white-collar crimes, and traffic and other violations. Looked at this broadly, "crime" is an economically important activity or "industry," notwithstanding the almost total neglect by economists. [^1] Some relevant evidence recently put together by the President's Commission on Law Enforcement and Administration of Justice (the "Crime Commission") is reproduced in Table 1. Public expenditures in 1965 at the federal, state, and local levels on police, criminal courts and counsel, and "corrections" amounted to over $\$ 4$ billion, while private outlays on burglar alarms, guards, counsel, and some other forms of protection were about $\$ 2$ billion. Unquestionably, public and especially private expenditures are significantly understated, since expenditures by many public agencies in the course of enforcing particular pieces of legislation, such as state fair-employment laws, [^2] are not included, and a myriad of private precautions against crime, ranging from suburban living to taxis, are also excluded.

尽管标题中使用了“犯罪”一词以减少术语创新，但分析的意图是足够广泛，以**涵盖所有违规行为**，而不仅仅是像谋杀、抢劫和攻击等重罪，这些罪行在报纸上受到大量报道，还包括逃税、所谓的白领犯罪以及交通和其他违规行为。**从这个广义上看，“犯罪”是一种经济上重要的活动或“产业”，尽管经济学家几乎完全忽视了这一点**。[1] 最近由总统执法与司法管理委员会（“犯罪委员会”）整理的一些相关证据在表1中列出。1965年，联邦、州和地方政府在警察、刑事法院和律师以及“矫正”方面的公共支出超过了40亿美元，而私人在入室盗窃警报、保安、律师和其他一些保护形式上的支出约为20亿美元。毫无疑问，公共支出，尤其是私人支出被显著低估，因为许多公共机构在执行特定立法（如州公平就业法）过程中产生的支出并未包括在内，而各种私人防范犯罪的措施，从郊区生活到出租车，也被排除在外。

The gross income from expenditures on various kinds of illegal consumption, including narcotics, prostitution, and mainly gambling, amounted to over $\$ 8$ billion. The value of crimes against property, including fraud, vandalism, and theft, amounted to almost $\$ 4$ billion, [^3] while about $\$ 3$ billion worth resulted from the loss of earnings due to homicide, assault, or other crimes. 表1还列出了犯罪委员会对各种犯罪直接成本的估计。各种非法消费（包括毒品、卖淫和主要的赌博）所产生的总收入超过了80亿美元。针对财产的犯罪（包括欺诈、破坏和盗窃）的价值接近40亿美元，[3] 而由于谋杀、攻击或其他犯罪造成的收入损失约为30亿美元。表中列出的所有成本总计约为210亿美元，几乎占1965年报告的国民收入的4%。如果将大量遗漏的部分计算在内，这一比例可能会更高。

Crime has probably become more important during the last forty years. The Crime Commission presents no evidence on trends in costs but does present evidence suggesting that the number of major felonies per capita has grown since the early thirties (President's Commission, 1967a, pp. 2231). Moreover, with the large growth of tax and other legislation, tax evasion and other kinds of white-collar crime have presumably grown much more rapidly than felonies. One piece of indirect evidence on the growth of crime is the large increase in the amount of currency in circulation since 1929. For sixty years prior to that date, the ratio of currency either to all money or to consumer expenditures had declined very substantially. Since then, in spite of further urbanization and income growth and the spread of credit cards and other kinds of credit, [^4] both ratios have increased sizeably. [^5] This reversal can be explained by an unusual increase in illegal activity, since currency has obvious advantages over checks in illegal transactions (the opposite is true for legal transactions) because no record of a transaction remains. [^6]
犯罪在过去四十年中可能变得更加重要。犯罪委员会没有提供关于成本趋势的证据，但确实提供了证据表明，自三十年代初以来，人均重大犯罪的数量有所增加（总统委员会，1967年a，2231页）。此外，随着税收和其他立法的快速增长，税收逃避和其他类型的白领犯罪显然增长得比重罪更快。关于犯罪增长的一个间接证据是，自1929年以来流通中的货币数量大幅增加。在此日期之前的六十年中，货币与所有货币或消费者支出之间的比例大幅下降。自那时以来，尽管城市化、收入增长以及信用卡和其他类型信用的普及，这两个比例都显著增加。这个逆转可以用非法活动的异常增加来解释，因为在非法交易中，货币相对于支票具有明显的优势（在合法交易中则相反），因为交易没有留下记录。

## B. The Model 

It is useful in determining how to combat crime in an optimal fashion to develop a model to incorporate the behavioral relations behind the costs listed in Table 1. These can be divided into five categories: the relations between (1) the number of crimes, called "offenses" in this essay, and the cost of offenses, (2) the number of offenses and the punishments meted out, (3) the number of offenses, arrests, and convictions and the public expenditures on police and courts, (4) the number of convictions and the costs of imprisonments or other kinds of punishments, and (5) the number of offenses and the private expenditures on protection and apprehension. The first four are discussed in turn, while the fifth is postponed until a later section.
在确定如何以最佳方式打击犯罪时，开发一个模型以纳入表1中列出的成本背后的行为关系是非常有用的。这些关系可以分为五类：（1）犯罪数量（在本文中称为“犯罪行为”）与犯罪成本之间的关系；（2）犯罪行为数量与施加的惩罚之间的关系；（3）犯罪行为数量、逮捕和定罪与公共在警察和法院上的支出之间的关系；（4）定罪数量与监禁或其他惩罚成本之间的关系；（5）犯罪行为数量与私人在保护和逮捕上的支出之间的关系。前四类关系将依次讨论，而第五类关系则推迟到后面的部分。

### 1. Damages

Usually a belief that other members of society are harmed is the motivation behind outlawing or otherwise restricting an activity. The amount of harm would tend to increase with the activity level, as in the relation
通常，认为社会其他成员受到伤害是禁止或限制某项活动的动机。随着活动水平的增加，伤害的程度往往也会增加，如同关系所示。

$$
H_{\mathrm{i}}=H_{\mathrm{i}}\left(O_{\mathrm{i}}\right)
$$

with

$$
H_{\mathrm{i}}^{\prime}=\frac{d H_{\mathrm{i}}}{d O_{\mathrm{i}}}>0
$$

where $H_{\mathrm{i}}$ is the harm from the $i$ th activity and $O_{\mathrm{i}}$ is the activity level. [^7] The concept of harm and the function relating its amount to the activity level are familiar to economists from their many discussions of activities causing external diseconomies. From this perspective, criminal activities are an important subset of the class of activities that cause diseconomies, with the level of criminal activities measured by the number of offenses.
其中 $H_{\mathrm{i}}$ 是第 $i$ 项活动造成的危害，$O_{\mathrm{i}}$ 是活动水平。[^7] 危害的概念以及其数量与活动水平之间的关系函数，对于经济学家来说并不陌生，因为他们在讨论导致外部不经济的活动时经常提到。从这个角度来看，犯罪活动是导致不经济的活动类别中的一个重要子集，犯罪活动的水平通过犯罪数量来衡量。

The social value of the gain to offenders presumably also tends to increase with the number of offenses, as in

$$
G=G(O)
$$
with
$$
G^{\prime}=\frac{d G}{d O}>0
$$

The net cost or damage to society is simply the difference between the harm and gain and can be written as

$$
D(O)=H(O)-G(O)
$$

If, as seems plausible, offenders usually eventually receive diminishing marginal gains and cause increasing marginal harm from additional offenses, $G^{\prime \prime}<0, H^{\prime \prime}>0$, and
如果，如此看似合理，违法者通常最终会获得递减的边际收益，并且因额外的犯罪行为造成递增的边际伤害，$G^{\prime \prime}<0, H^{\prime \prime}>0$，并且

$$
D^{\prime \prime}=H^{\prime \prime}-G^{\prime \prime}>0
$$

which is an important condition used later in the analysis of optimality positions (see, for example, the Mathematical Appendix). Since both $H^{\prime}$ and $G^{\prime}>0$, the sign of $D^{\prime}$ depends on their relative magnitudes. It follows from (4), however, that
这是在后续的最优位置分析中使用的重要条件（例如，参见数学附录）。由于 $H^{\prime}$ 和 $G^{\prime}>0$，因此 $D^{\prime}$ 的符号取决于它们的相对大小。然而，从（4）可以得出：

$$
D^{\prime}(O)>0 \text { for all } O>O_{\mathrm{a}} \text { if } D^{\prime}\left(O_{\mathrm{a}}\right) \geq 0
$$

Until Section V the discussion is restricted to the region where $D^{\prime}>0$, the region providing the strongest justification for outlawing an activity. In that section the general problem of external diseconomies is reconsidered from our viewpoint, and there $D^{\prime}<0$ is also permitted.

在第五节之前，讨论仅限于$D^{\prime}>0$的区域，这一区域为禁止某项活动提供了最有力的理由。在该节中，我们将从我们的视角重新审视外部不经济性的一般问题，并且在此也允许$D^{\prime}<0$。

The top part of Table 1 lists costs of various crimes, which have been interpreted by us as estimates of the value of resources used up in these crimes. These values are important components of, but are not identical to, the net damages to society. For example, the cost of murder is measured by the loss in earnings of victims and excludes, among other things, the value placed by society on life itself; the cost of gambling excludes both the utility to those gambling and the "external" disutility to some clergy and others; the cost of "transfers" like burglary and embezzlement excludes social attitudes toward forced wealth redistributions and also the effects on capital accumulation of the possibility of theft. Consequently, the $\$ 15$ billion estimate for the cost of crime in Table 1 may be a significant understatement of the net damages to society, not only because the costs of many white-collar crimes are omitted, but also because much of the damage is omitted even for the crimes covered.

表1的顶部列出了各种犯罪的成本，我们将其解读为这些犯罪中消耗的资源价值的估算。这些价值是社会净损害的重要组成部分，但并不完全相同。例如，谋杀的成本是通过受害者的收入损失来衡量的，排除了社会对生命本身的价值等其他因素；赌博的成本则排除了赌博者的效用以及一些神职人员和其他人所感受到的“外部”不效用；像入室盗窃和挪用公款这样的“转移”成本则排除了社会对强制财富再分配的态度，以及盗窃可能对资本积累的影响。因此，表1中对犯罪成本的150亿美元估算可能显著低估了对社会的净损害，这不仅是因为许多白领犯罪的成本被遗漏，还因为即使是涵盖的犯罪，许多损害也被忽略了。

### 2. The Cost of Apprehension and Conviction 

The more that is spent on policemen, court personnel, and specialized equipment, the easier it is to discover offenses and convict offenders. One can postulate a relation between the output of police and court "activity" and various inputs of manpower, materials, and capital, as in $A=f(m, r, c)$, where $f$ is a production function summarizing the "state of the arts." Given $f$ and input prices, increased "activity" would be more costly, as summarized by the relation
花费在警察、法庭人员和专业设备上的资金越多，发现犯罪和定罪的难度就越小。可以假设警察和法庭“活动”的产出与人力、物资和资本等各种投入之间存在关系，如 $A=f(m, r, c)$，其中 $f$ 是总结“技术状态”的生产函数。考虑到 $f$ 和投入价格，增加“活动”的成本将更高，这可以通过以下关系来概括。
$$
C=C(A)
$$
and
$$
C^{\prime}=\frac{d C}{d A}>0
$$

It would be cheaper to achieve any given level of activity the cheaper were policemen, [^8] judges, counsel, and juries and the more highly developed the state of the arts, as determined by technologies like fingerprinting, wiretapping, computer control, and lie-detecting. [^9]
如果警察、法官、律师和陪审团的成本更低，而技术如指纹识别、窃听、计算机控制和测谎等艺术水平更高，那么实现任何特定活动水平的成本将会更低。

One approximation to an empirical measure of "activity" is the number of offenses cleared by conviction. It can be written as
一种对“活动”的经验性衡量的近似方法是通过定罪清除的犯罪数量。可以表示为

$$
A \cong p O
$$

where $p$, the ratio of offenses cleared by convictions to all offenses, is the over-all probability that an offense is cleared by conviction. By substituting (7) into (6) and differentiating, one has
其中 $p$ 是通过定罪清除的犯罪与所有犯罪的比率，是通过定罪清除犯罪的总体概率。通过将 (7) 代入 (6) 并进行微分，可以得到

$$
C_{p}=\frac{\partial C(p O)}{\partial p}=C^{\prime} O>0
$$

and

$$
C_{o}=C^{\prime} p>0
$$

if $p O \neq 0$. An increase in either the probability of conviction or the number of offenses would increase total costs. If the marginal cost of increased "activity" were rising, further implications would be that
如果 $p O \neq 0$。无论是定罪的概率增加还是犯罪次数的增加，都会导致总成本上升。如果增加“活动”的边际成本在上升，那么进一步的含义将是

$$
\begin{aligned}
& C_{p p}=C^{\prime \prime} O^{2}>0 \\
& C_{o o}=C^{\prime \prime} p^{2}>0
\end{aligned}
$$

and

$$
C_{p o}=C_{o p}=C^{\prime \prime} p O+C^{\prime}>0
$$

A more sophisticated and realistic approach drops the implication of (7) that convictions alone measure "activity," or even that $p$ and $O$ have identical elasticities, and introduces the more general relation
一种更复杂和现实的方法摒弃了（7）所暗示的仅凭信念来衡量“活动”，甚至$p$和$O$具有相同弹性的观点，并引入了更一般的关系。

$$
A=h(p, O, a)
$$

The variable $a$ stands for arrests and other determinants of "activity," and there is no presumption that the elasticity of $h$ with respect to $p$ equals that with respect to $O$. Substitution yields the cost function $C=C(p, O, a)$. If, as is extremely likely, $h_{p}, h_{o}$, and $h_{a}$ are all greater than zero, then clearly $C_{p}, C_{o}$, and $C_{a}$ are all greater than zero.
变量 $a$ 代表逮捕和其他“活动”的决定因素，并且没有假设 $h$ 对 $p$ 的弹性等于对 $O$ 的弹性。替换后得到成本函数 $C=C(p, O, a)$。如果 $h_{p}, h_{o}$ 和 $h_{a}$ 都大于零，这种情况极有可能，那么显然 $C_{p}, C_{o}$ 和 $C_{a}$ 也都大于零. 

In order to insure that optimality positions do not lie at "corners," it is necessary to place some restrictions on the second derivatives of the cost function. Combined with some other assumptions, it is sufficient that
为了确保最优位置不位于“角落”，有必要对成本函数的二阶导数施加一些限制。结合其他一些假设，只需满足以下条件：

$$
\begin{aligned}
& C_{p p} \geq 0 \\
& C_{o o} \geq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
and
$$
C_{p o} \cong 0
$$

(see the Mathematical Appendix). The first two restrictions are rather plausible, the third much less so. [^10]
（见数学附录）。前两个限制相当合理，而第三个限制则不那么可信。[^10]

Table 1 indicates that in 1965 public expenditures in the United States on police and courts totaled more than $\$ 3$ billion, by no means a minor item. Separate estimates were prepared for each of seven major felonies. [^11] Expenditures on them averaged about $\$ 500$ per offense (reported) and about $\$ 2,000$ per person arrested, with almost $\$ 1,000$ being spent per murder (President's Commission, 1967a, pp. 264-65); \$500 is an estimate of the average cost

$$
A C=\frac{C(p, O, a)}{O}
$$

of these felonies and would presumably be a larger figure if the number of either arrests or convictions were greater. Marginal costs $\left(C_{0}\right)$ would be at least $\$ 500$ if condition (11), $C_{o o} \geq 0$, were assumed to hold throughout.

表1显示，1965年美国在警察和法院上的公共支出总额超过30亿美元，这绝不是一个小数目。针对七种主要重罪分别进行了单独估算。对这些重罪的支出平均约为每起犯罪（报告）500美元，每名被捕者约为2000美元，谋杀案的支出几乎为1000美元（总统委员会，1967年，264-265页）；500美元是这些重罪的平均成本估算

$$
A C=\frac{C(p, O, a)}{O}
$$

如果逮捕或定罪的数量更大，这个数字可能会更高。边际成本 $\left(C_{0}\right)$ 至少为500美元，如果假设条件（11），$C_{o o} \geq 0$，在整个过程中成立。
### 3. The Supply of Offenses 

Theories about the determinants of the number of offenses differ greatly, from emphasis on skull types and biological inheritance to family upbringing and disenchantment with society. Practically all the diverse theories agree, however, that when other variables are held constant, an increase in a person's probability of conviction or punishment if convicted would generally decrease, perhaps substantially, perhaps negligibly, the number of offenses he commits. In addition, a common generalization by persons with judicial experience is that a change in the probability has a greater effect on the number of offenses than a change in the punishment, [^12] although, as far as I can tell, none of the prominent theories shed any light on this relation.
关于犯罪数量决定因素的理论差异很大，从强调头骨类型和生物遗传到家庭教育和对社会的失望。然而，几乎所有不同的理论都一致认为，当其他变量保持不变时，一个人被定罪或被判刑的概率增加，通常会减少他所犯罪的数量，可能是显著减少，也可能是微不足道的。**此外，具有司法经验的人普遍认为，概率的变化对犯罪数量的影响大于惩罚的变化，尽管据我所知，没有任何显著的理论对此关系提供了明确的解释**。

The approach taken here follows the economists' usual analysis of choice and assumes that a person commits an offense if the expected utility to him exceeds the utility he could get by using his time and other resources at other activities. Some persons become "criminals," therefore, not because their basic motivation differs from that of other persons, but because their benefits and costs differ. I cannot pause to discuss the many general implications of this approach, [^13] except to remark that criminal behavior becomes part of a much more general theory and does not require ad hoc concepts of differential association, anomie, and the like, [^14] nor does it assume perfect knowledge, lightening-fast calculation, or any of the other caricatures of economic theory.
这里采用的方法遵循经济学家对选择的常规分析，并假设如果一个人所期望的效用超过他通过将时间和其他资源用于其他活动所能获得的效用，那么他就会犯下罪行。因此，一些人之所以成为“罪犯”，并不是因为他们的基本动机与其他人不同，而是因为他们的收益和成本不同。我无法停下来讨论这种方法的许多一般性含义，[^13] 只想指出，犯罪行为成为一个更为普遍理论的一部分，并不需要临时的差异关联、失范等概念，[^14] 也不假设完美的知识、闪电般的计算或经济理论的其他夸张表现。

This approach implies that there is a function relating the number of offenses by any person to his probability of conviction, to his punishment if convicted, and to other variables, such as the income available to him in legal and other illegal activities, the frequency of nuisance arrests, and his willingness to commit an illegal act. This can be represented as
这种方法意味着存在一个函数，将任何人的犯罪次数与其被定罪的概率、被定罪后的惩罚以及其他变量（例如他在合法和其他非法活动中可获得的收入、滋扰性逮捕的频率以及他愿意实施非法行为的程度）联系起来。这可以表示为

$$
O_{j}=O_{j}\left(p_{j}, f_{j}, u_{j}\right)
$$

where $O_{j}$ is the number of offenses he would commit during a particular period, $p_{j}$ his probability of conviction per offense, $f_{j}$ his punishment per offense, and $u_{j}$ a portmanteau variable representing all these other influences. [^15]
其中 $O_{j}$ 是他在特定时期内可能犯下的罪行数量，$p_{j}$ 是每次犯罪被定罪的概率，$f_{j}$ 是每次犯罪的惩罚，$u_{j}$ 是一个综合变量，代表所有其他影响因素。 [^15]

Since only convicted offenders are punished, in effect there is "price discrimination" and uncertainty: if convicted, he pays $f_{j}$ per convicted offense, while otherwise he does not. An increase in either $p_{j}$ or $f_{j}$ would reduce the utility expected from an offense and thus would tend to reduce the number of offenses because either the probability of "paying" the higher "price" or the "price" itself would increase. [^16] That is,
由于只有被定罪的罪犯会受到惩罚，因此实际上存在“价格歧视”和不确定性：如果被定罪，他每次被定罪的罪行需支付 $f_{j}$，而如果没有被定罪则不需要支付。无论是 $p_{j}$ 还是 $f_{j}$ 的增加都会降低从犯罪中预期的效用，因此会倾向于减少犯罪数量，因为“支付”更高“价格”的概率或“价格”本身都会增加。[^16] 也就是说，

$$
O_{p_{j}}=\frac{\partial O_{j}}{\partial p_{j}}<0
$$
and

$$
O_{f_{j}}=\frac{\partial O_{j}}{\partial f_{j}}<0
$$

which are the generally accepted restrictions mentioned above. The effect of changes in some components of $u_{j}$ could also be anticipated. For example, a rise in the income available in legal activities or an increase in law-abidingness due, say, to "education" would reduce the incentive to enter illegal activities and thus would reduce the number of offenses. Or a shift in the form of the punishment, say, from a fine to imprisonment,
上述一般公认的限制。某些 $u_{j}$ 组件变化的影响也可以预见。例如，合法活动中可用收入的增加或由于“教育”而导致的守法意识增强，会减少进入非法活动的动机，从而减少犯罪数量。或者，惩罚形式的变化，例如，从罚款转为监禁，

$$
E U_{j}=p_{j} U_{j}\left(Y_{j}-f_{j}\right)+\left(1-p_{j}\right) U_{j}\left(Y_{j}\right)
$$

where $Y_{j}$ is his income, monetary plus psychic, from an offense; $U_{j}$ is his utility function; and $f_{j}$ is to be interpreted as the monetary equivalent of the punishment. Then
其中 $Y_{j}$ 是他因犯罪所获得的收入，包括金钱和心理上的收益；$U_{j}$ 是他的效用函数；$f_{j}$ 应被理解为惩罚的货币等价物。然后

$$
\frac{\partial E U_{j}}{\partial p_{j}}=U_{j}\left(Y_{j}-f_{j}\right)-U_{j}\left(Y_{j}\right)<0
$$

and

$$
\frac{\partial E U_{j}}{\partial f_{j}}=-p_{j} U_{j}'\left(Y_{j}-f_{j}\right)<0
$$

as long as the marginal utility of income is positive. One could expand the analysis by incorporating the costs and probabilities of arrests, detentions, and trials that do not result in conviction. would tend to reduce the number of offenses, at least temporarily, because they cannot be committed while in prison.
只要收入的边际效用是正的。可以通过纳入逮捕、拘留和未导致定罪的审判的成本和概率来扩展分析。这将倾向于减少犯罪数量，至少在短期内，因为在监狱中无法实施犯罪。

This approach also has an interesting interpretation of the presumed greater response to a change in the probability than in the punishment. An increase in $p_{j}$ "compensated" by an equal percentage reduction in $f_{j}$ would not change the expected income from an offense [^17] but could change the expected utility, because the amount of risk would change. It is easily shown that an increase in $p_{j}$ would reduce the expected utility, and thus the number of offenses, more than an equal percentage increase in $f_{j}$[^18] if $j$ has preference for risk; the increase in $f_{j}$ would have the greater effect if he has aversion to risk; and they would have the same effect if he is risk neutral. [^19] The widespread generalization that offenders are more deterred by the probability of conviction than by the punishment when convicted turns out to imply in the expected-utility approach that offenders are risk preferrers, at least in the relevant region of punishments.

这种方法对假定的对概率变化的反应大于对惩罚变化的反应有一个有趣的解释。$p_{j}$ 的增加如果通过 $f_{j}$ 的等比例减少来“补偿”，将不会改变犯罪的预期收入 [^17]，但可能会改变预期效用，因为风险的大小会发生变化。可以很容易地证明，如果 $j$ 偏好风险，$p_{j}$ 的增加将比 $f_{j}$ 的等比例增加更大程度地降低预期效用，从而减少犯罪数量；如果他厌恶风险，$f_{j}$ 的增加将产生更大的影响；如果他对风险中立，则两者的影响将相同。[^19] 广泛的概括认为，罪犯更容易受到定罪概率的威慑，而不是被定罪后的惩罚，实际上在预期效用方法中意味着罪犯是风险偏好者，至少在相关的惩罚范围内。

The total number of offenses is the sum of all the $O_{j}$ and would depend on the set of $p_{j}, f_{j}$, and $u_{j}$. Although these variables are likely to differ significantly between persons because of differences in intelligence, age, education, previous offense history, wealth, family upbringing, etc., for simplicity I now consider only their average values, $p, f$, and $u,{ }^{20}$ and write the market offense function as
总犯罪次数是所有 $O_{j}$ 的总和，并且取决于 $p_{j}, f_{j}$ 和 $u_{j}$ 的集合。尽管由于智力、年龄、教育、以往犯罪历史、财富、家庭背景等方面的差异，这些变量在不同人之间可能会有显著差异，但为了简化起见，我现在只考虑它们的平均值 $p, f$ 和 $u,{ }^{20}$，并将市场犯罪函数写为

$$
O=O(p, f, u)
$$

This function is assumed to have the same kinds of properties as the individual functions, in particular, to be negatively related to $p$ and $f$ and to be more responsive to the former than the latter if, and only if, offenders on balance have risk preference. Smigel (1965) and Ehrlich (1967) estimate functions like (14) for seven felonies reported by the Federal Bureau of Investigation using state data as the basic unit of observation. They find that the relations are quite stable, as evidenced by high correlation coefficients; that there are significant negative effects on $O$ of $p$ and $f$; and that usually the effect of $p$ exceeds that of $f$, indicating preference for risk in the region of observation.
这个函数被假定具有与个别函数相同的性质，特别是与 $p$ 和 $f$ 之间存在负相关关系，并且仅当且仅当犯罪者总体上具有风险偏好时，对前者的反应比对后者更为敏感。Smigel (1965) 和 Ehrlich (1967) 使用州数据作为基本观察单位，估计了联邦调查局报告的七种重罪的类似于 (14) 的函数。他们发现这些关系相当稳定，相关系数很高；$p$ 和 $f$ 对 $O$ 产生显著的负面影响；而且通常 $p$ 的影响超过 $f$，这表明在观察区域内存在风险偏好。

A well-known result states that, in equilibrium, the real incomes of persons in risky activities are, at the margin, relatively high or low as persons are generally risk avoiders or preferrers. If offenders were risk preferrers, this implies that the real income of offenders would be lower, at the margin, than the incomes they could receive in less risky legal activities, and conversely if they were risk avoiders. Whether "crime pays" is then an implication of the attitudes offenders have toward risk and is not directly related to the efficiency of the police or the amount spent on combatting crime. If, however, risk were preferred at some values of $p$ and $f$ and disliked at others, public policy could influence whether "crime pays" by its choice of $p$ and $f$. Indeed, it is shown later that the social loss from illegal activities is usually minimized by selecting $p$ and $f$ in regions where risk is preferred, that is, in regions where "crime does not pay."
一个众所周知的结果表明，在均衡状态下，从事风险活动的人的实际收入在边际上相对较高或较低，因为人们通常是风险规避者或风险偏好者。如果犯罪者是风险偏好者，这意味着犯罪者的实际收入在边际上会低于他们在较少风险的合法活动中可以获得的收入，反之亦然，如果他们是风险规避者。那么，“犯罪是否有利可图”是犯罪者对风险态度的一个体现，并不直接与警察的效率或打击犯罪的支出金额相关。然而，如果在某些$p$和$f$的值下风险被偏好，而在其他值下被厌恶，公共政策可以通过选择$p$和$f$来影响“犯罪是否有利可图”。实际上，后面将证明，非法活动造成的社会损失通常通过选择在风险被偏好的区域，即“犯罪不获利”的区域的$p$和$f$来最小化。

### 4. Punishments 

Mankind has invented a variety of ingenious punishments to inflict on convicted offenders: death, torture, branding, fines, imprisonment, banishment, restrictions on movement and occupation, and loss of citizenship are just the more common ones. In the United States, less serious offenses are punished primarily by fines, supplemented occasionally by probation, petty restrictions like temporary suspension of one's driver's license, and imprisonment. The more serious offenses are punished by a combination of probation, imprisonment, parole, fines, and various restrictions on choice of occupation. A recent survey estimated for an average day in 1965 the number of persons who were either on probation, parole, or institutionalized in a jail or juvenile home (President's Commission 1967b). The total number of persons in one of these categories came to about $1,300,000$, which is about 2 per cent of the labor force. About one-half were on probation, one-third were institutionalized, and the remaining one-sixth were on parole.
人类发明了各种巧妙的惩罚方式来惩治被定罪的罪犯：死刑、酷刑、烙印、罚款、监禁、流放、限制行动和职业选择，以及剥夺公民权等都是比较常见的。在美国，较轻的罪行主要通过罚款来惩罚，偶尔会辅以缓刑、临时吊销驾驶执照等小限制，以及监禁。较严重的罪行则通过缓刑、监禁、假释、罚款以及对职业选择的各种限制来惩罚。最近的一项调查估计，在1965年的一个平均日子里，处于缓刑、假释或被关押在监狱或少年之家的人数（总统委员会1967b）。这些类别中的总人数约为1,300,000人，约占劳动力的2%。其中大约一半处于缓刑，三分之一被关押，剩下的六分之一处于假释状态。

The cost of different punishments to an offender can be made comparable by converting them into their monetary equivalent or worth, which, of course, is directly measured only for fines. For example, the cost of an imprisonment is the discounted sum of the earnings foregone and the value placed on the restrictions in consumption and freedom. Since the earnings foregone and the value placed on prison restrictions vary from person to person, the cost even of a prison sentence of given duration is not a unique quantity but is generally greater, for example, to offenders who could earn more outside of prison. [^21] The cost to each offender would be greater the longer the prison sentence, since both foregone earnings and foregone consumption are positively related to the length of sentences.
不同惩罚对罪犯的成本可以通过将其转换为货币等值或价值来进行比较，当然，这种直接测量仅适用于罚款。例如，监禁的成本是放弃收入的折现总和以及对消费和自由限制所赋予的价值。由于放弃的收入和对监禁限制的价值因人而异，即使是给定期限的监禁刑罚，其成本也不是一个唯一的量，通常对于那些在监狱外能够赚取更多收入的罪犯来说，成本更高。[^21] 对每个罪犯来说，监禁刑期越长，成本就越高，因为放弃的收入和放弃的消费与刑期长度呈正相关。

Punishments affect not only offenders but also other members of society. Aside from collection costs, fines paid by offenders are received as revenue by others. Most punishments, however, hurt other members as well as offenders: for example, imprisonment requires expenditures on guards, supervisory personnel, buildings, food, etc. Currently about $\$ 1$ billion is being spent each year in the United States on probation, parole, and institutionalization alone, with the daily cost per case varying tremendously from a low of $\$ 0.38$ for adults on probation to a high of $\$ 11.00$ for juveniles in detention institutions (President's Commission, 1967b, pp. 193-94).
惩罚不仅影响违法者，还影响社会的其他成员。除了收集成本外，违法者支付的罚款也被其他人视为收入。然而，大多数惩罚不仅伤害违法者，也伤害其他成员：例如，监禁需要在看守、监督人员、建筑、食品等方面支出。目前，美国每年在缓刑、假释和监禁方面的支出约为10亿美元，每个案件的每日成本差异巨大，从成年缓刑者的最低0.38美元到青少年拘留机构的最高11.00美元不等（总统委员会，1967年b，页193-194）。

The total social cost of punishments is the cost to offenders plus the cost or minus the gain to others. Fines produce a gain to the latter that equals the cost to offenders, aside from collection costs, and so the social cost of fines is about zero, as befits a transfer payment. The social cost of probation, imprisonment, and other punishments, however, generally exceeds that to offenders, because others are also hurt. The derivation of optimality conditions in the next section is made more convenient if social costs are written in terms of offender costs as

$$
f^{\prime} \equiv b f
$$

where $f^{\prime}$ is the social cost and $b$ is a coefficient that transforms $f$ into $f^{\prime}$. The size of $b$ varies greatly between different kinds of punishments: $b \cong 0$ for fines, while $b>1$ for torture, probation, parole, imprisonment, and most other punishments. It is especially large for juveniles in detention homes or for adults in prisons and is rather close to unity for torture or for adults on parole.
惩罚的总社会成本是违法者的成本加上他人的成本或减去他人的收益。罚款在扣除征收成本后，给他人带来的收益等于违法者的成本，因此罚款的社会成本几乎为零，这符合转移支付的性质。然而，缓刑、监禁和其他惩罚的社会成本通常超过违法者的成本，因为其他人也会受到伤害。如果将社会成本以违法者成本的形式表示，下一节中推导最优条件将更加方便，即 $f^{\prime} \equiv b f$, 其中，$f^{\prime}$ 表示社会成本，$b$ 是将 $f$ 转换为 $f^{\prime}$ 的系数。$b$ 的大小在不同类型的惩罚之间差异很大：对于罚款，$b \cong 0$；而对于酷刑、缓刑、假释、监禁和大多数其他惩罚，$b>1$。对于拘留所中的未成年人或监狱中的成年人，$b$ 尤其大；而对于酷刑或假释中的成年人，$b$ 则接近于1。

# III. Optimality Conditions 

The relevant parameters and behavioral functions have been introduced, and the stage is set for a discussion of social policy. If the aim simply were deterrence, the probability of conviction, $p$, could be raised close to 1 , and punishments, $f$, could be made to exceed the gain: in this way the number of offenses, $O$, could be reduced almost at will. However, an increase in $p$ increases the social cost of offenses through its effect on the cost of combatting offenses, $C$, as does an increase in $f$ if $b>0$ through the effect on the cost of punishments, $b f$. At relatively modest values of $p$ and $f$, these effects might outweigh the social gain from increased deterrence. Similarly, if the aim simply were to make "the punishment fit the crime," $p$ could be set close to 1 , and $f$ could be equated to the harm imposed on the rest of society. Again, however, such a policy ignores the social cost of increases in $p$ and $f$.

相关参数与行为函数已引入，社会政策讨论的舞台就此铺就。若仅以威慑为目的，可将定罪概率$p$提升至接近1，并使惩罚力度$f$超过犯罪收益：如此便能近乎随意地减少犯罪数量$O$。然而，$p$值的增加会通过影响打击犯罪的成本$C$而提升社会成本；当$b>0$时，$f$值的增加同样会通过惩罚成本$b f$产生类似效果。在$p$和$f$取值相对适中时，这些效应可能压倒威慑增强带来的社会收益。**同理，若仅追求"罪罚相当"，可将$p$设为接近1，并使$f$等同于犯罪行为对社会造成的损害。但此种政策再次忽视了提升$p$与$f$值所产生的社会成本。**

What is needed is a criterion that goes beyond catchy phrases and gives due weight to the damages from offenses, the costs of apprehending and convicting offenders, and the social cost of punishments. The socialwelfare function of modern welfare economics is such a criterion, and one might assume that society has a function that measures the social loss from offenses. If
所需的是一个超越吸引人眼球的标语、并充分考量以下因素的标准：违法行为造成的损害、抓捕与定罪罪犯的成本，以及刑罚的社会代价。现代福利经济学中的社会福利函数正是这样一种标准，我们可以假设社会存在一个衡量违法行为所造成社会损失的函数。如果

$$
L=L(D, C, b f, O)
$$

is the function measuring social loss, with presumably

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial D}>0, \quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial C}>0, \quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial b f}>0
$$

the aim would be to select values of $f, C$, and possibly $b$ that minimize $L$.

It is more convenient and transparent, however, to develop the discussion at this point in terms of a less general formulation, namely, to assume that the loss function is identical with the total social loss in real income from offenses, convictions, and punishments, as in
目标是选择 $f, C$ 和可能的 $b$ 的值，以最小化 $L$。然而，在这一点上，以一个较不一般的形式来展开讨论会更方便和清晰，即假设损失函数与因犯罪、定罪和惩罚造成的实际收入的总社会损失相同，如下所示。

$$
L=D(O)+C(p, O)+b p f O
$$

The term $b p f O$ is the total social loss from punishments, since $b f$ is the loss per offense punished and $p O$ is the number of offenses punished (if there are a fairly large number of independent offenses). The variables directly subject to social control are the amounts spent in combatting offenses, $C$; the punishment per offense for those convicted, $f$; and the form of punishments, summarized by $b$. Once chosen, these variables, via the $D, C$, and $O$ functions, indirectly determine $p, O, D$, and ultimately the loss $L$.
术语 $b p f O$ 是惩罚所造成的总社会损失，因为 $b f$ 是每次被惩罚的犯罪所造成的损失，而 $p O$ 是被惩罚的犯罪数量（如果有相当多的独立犯罪）。直接受社会控制的变量包括用于打击犯罪的支出 $C$；对被定罪者每次犯罪的惩罚 $f$；以及惩罚的形式，由 $b$ 概括。一旦选择了这些变量，它们通过 $D, C$ 和 $O$ 函数间接决定了 $p, O, D$，并最终决定了损失 $L$。

Analytical convenience suggests that $p$ rather than $C$ be considered a decision variable. Also, the coefficient $b$ is assumed in this section to be a given constant greater than zero. Then $p$ and $f$ are the only decision variables, and their optimal values are found by differentiating $L$ to find the two first-order optimality conditions, [^22]
分析的便利性表明，应将$p$而不是$C$视为决策变量。此外，本节假设系数$b$是一个大于零的给定常数。那么$p$和$f$是唯一的决策变量，通过对$L$进行微分来找到两个一阶最优性条件，从而确定它们的最优值。[^22]

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial f}=D^{\prime} O_{f}+C^{\prime} O_{f}+b p f O_{f}+b p O=0
$$

and

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial p}=D^{\prime} O_{p}+C^{\prime} O_{p}+C_{p}+b p f O_{p}+b f O=0
$$

If $O_{f}$ and $O_{p}$ are not equal to zero, one can divide through by them, and recombine terms, to get the more interesting expressions

$$
D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}=-b p f\left(1-\frac{1}{\varepsilon_{f}}\right)
$$

and

$$
D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}+C_{p} \frac{1}{O_{p}}=-b p f\left(1-\frac{1}{\varepsilon_{p}}\right)
$$

where

$$
\varepsilon_{f}=-\frac{f}{O} O_{f}
$$

and

$$
\varepsilon_{p}=-\frac{p}{O} O_{p}
$$

The term on the left side of each equation gives the marginal cost of increasing the number of offenses, $O$ : in equation (21) through a reduction in $f$ and in (22) through a reduction in $p$. Since $C^{\prime}>0$ and $O$ is assumed to be in a region where $D^{\prime}>0$, the marginal cost of increasing $O$ through $f$ must be positive. A reduction in $p$ partly reduces the cost of combatting offenses, and, therefore, the marginal cost of increasing $O$ must be less when $p$ rather than when $f$ is reduced (see Fig. 1); the former could even be negative if $C_{p}$ were sufficiently large. Average "revenue," given by $-b p f$, is negative, but marginal revenue, given by the right-hand side of equations (21) and (22), is not necessarily negative and would be positive if the elasticities $\varepsilon_{p}$ and $\varepsilon_{f}$ were less than unity. Since the loss is minimized when marginal revenue equals marginal cost (see Fig. 1), the optimal value of $\varepsilon_{f}$ must be less than unity, and that of $\varepsilon_{p}$ could only exceed unity if $C_{p}$ were sufficiently large. This is a reversal of the usual equilibrium condition for an income-maximizing firm, which is that the elasticity of demand must exceed unity, because in the usual case average revenue is assumed to be positive. [^23]
每个方程左侧的项给出了增加犯罪数量 $O$ 的边际成本：在方程 (21) 中通过减少 $f$，在方程 (22) 中通过减少 $p$。由于 $C^{\prime}>0$，并且假设 $O$ 处于 $D^{\prime}>0$ 的区域，因此通过 $f$ 增加 $O$ 的边际成本必须是正的。减少 $p$ 部分降低了打击犯罪的成本，因此，当减少 $p$ 而不是 $f$ 时，增加 $O$ 的边际成本必须更低（见图 1）；如果 $C_{p}$ 足够大，前者甚至可能为负。平均“收入”，由 $-b p f$ 给出，是负的，但边际收入，由方程 (21) 和 (22) 的右侧给出，并不一定是负的，如果弹性 $\varepsilon_{p}$ 和 $\varepsilon_{f}$ 小于单位，则会是正的。由于当边际收入等于边际成本时损失最小化（见图 1），因此 $\varepsilon_{f}$ 的最优值必须小于单位，而 $\varepsilon_{p}$ 的值只有在 $C_{p}$ 足够大时才能超过单位。这是收入最大化企业通常均衡条件的逆转，通常情况下，需求的弹性必须超过单位，因为在通常情况下，平均收入被假设为正的。[^23]
![[Pasted image 20250326010449.png]]


Since the marginal cost of changing $O$ through a change in $p$ is less than that of changing $O$ through $f$, the equilibrium marginal revenue from $p$ must also be less than that from $f$. But equations (21) and (22) indicate that the marginal revenue from $p$ can be less if, and only if, $\varepsilon_{p}>\varepsilon_{f}$. As pointed out earlier, however, this is precisely the condition indicating that offenders have preference for risk and thus that "crime does not pay." Consequently, the loss from offenses is minimized if $p$ and $f$ are selected from those regions where offenders are, on balance, risk preferrers. Although only the attitudes offenders have toward risk can directly determine whether "crime pays," rational public policy indirectly insures that "crime does not pay" through its choice of $p$ and $f .{ }^{24}$
由于通过改变 $p$ 来改变 $O$ 的边际成本低于通过 $f$ 来改变 $O$ 的边际成本，因此来自 $p$ 的均衡边际收益也必须低于来自 $f$ 的边际收益。但方程（21）和（22）表明，只有当 $\varepsilon_{p}>\varepsilon_{f}$ 时，来自 $p$ 的边际收益才可能更低。然而，如前所述，这正是表明违法者偏好风险的条件，因此“犯罪没有好处”。因此，如果 $p$ 和 $f$ 选择自那些违法者总体上偏好风险的区域，违法行为造成的损失将被最小化。尽管只有违法者对风险的态度可以直接决定“犯罪是否有利”，理性的公共政策通过选择 $p$ 和 $f$ 间接确保“犯罪没有好处”。

I indicated earlier that the actual $p$ 's and $f$ 's for major felonies in the United States generally seem to be in regions where the effect (measured by elasticity) of $p$ on offenses exceeds that of $f$, that is, where offenders are risk preferrers and "crime does not pay" (Smigel, 1965; Ehrlich, 1967). Moreover, both elasticities are generally less than unity. In both respects, therefore, actual public policy is consistent with the implications of the optimality analysis.
我之前提到过，美国重大犯罪的实际 $p$ 和 $f$ 通常似乎处于这样的区域：在这些区域中，$p$ 对犯罪的影响（以弹性衡量）超过 $f$ 的影响，也就是说，犯罪者更倾向于冒险，并且“犯罪没有好处”（Smigel, 1965; Ehrlich, 1967）。此外，这两种弹性通常都小于1。因此，在这两个方面，实际的公共政策与最优性分析的含义是一致的。

If the supply of offenses depended only on pf-offenders were risk neutral-a reduction in $p$ "compensated" by an equal percentage increase in $f$ would leave unchanged $p f, O, D(O)$, and $b p f O$ but would reduce the loss, because the costs of apprehension and conviction would be lowered by the reduction in $p$. The loss would be minimized, therefore, by lowering $p$ arbitrarily close to zero and raising $f$ sufficiently high so that the product $p f$ would induce the optimal number of offenses. ${ }^{25} \mathrm{~A}$ fortiori, if offenders were risk avoiders, the loss would be minimized by setting $p$ arbitrarily close to zero, for a "compensated" reduction in $p$ reduces not only $C$ but also $O$ and thus $D$ and $b p f O .{ }^{26}$
如果犯罪的供给仅依赖于风险中立的犯罪者，那么$p$的减少通过$f$的等比例增加“补偿”后，将不会改变$p f, O, D(O)$和$b p f O$，但会减少损失，因为逮捕和定罪的成本会因$p$的降低而降低。因此，通过将$p$降低到接近零并将$f$提高到足够高的水平，以使得乘积$p f$能够引发最佳数量的犯罪，损失将被最小化。更进一步，如果犯罪者是风险规避者，那么通过将$p$降低到接近零来最小化损失，因为“补偿”后的$p$减少不仅降低了$C$，还降低了$O$，从而影响$D$和$b p f O$。

There was a tendency during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries in Anglo-Saxon countries, and even today in many Communist and underdeveloped countries, to punish those convicted of criminal offenses rather severely, at the same time that the probability of capture and conviction was set at rather low values. [^27] A promising explanation of this tendency is that an increased probability of conviction obviously absorbs public and private resources in the form of more policemen, judges, juries, and so forth. Consequently, a "compensated" reduction in this probability obviously reduces expenditures on combatting crime, and, since the expected punishment is unchanged, there is no "obvious" offsetting increase in either the amount of damages or the cost of punishments. The result can easily be continuous political pressure to keep police and other expenditures relatively low and to compensate by meting out strong punishments to those convicted.
在十八和十九世纪的盎格鲁-撒克逊国家，甚至在今天的许多共产主义和欠发达国家，普遍存在一种倾向，即对被定罪的犯罪者施以相当严厉的惩罚，同时被捕和定罪的概率却相对较低。[^27] 对这一倾向的一个有希望的解释是，增加定罪的概率显然会消耗公共和私人资源，例如更多的警察、法官、陪审团等。因此，显然通过“补偿性”降低这一概率可以减少打击犯罪的支出，而由于预期的惩罚没有改变，因此在赔偿金额或惩罚成本上没有“明显”的抵消性增加。结果可能导致持续的政治压力，以保持警察和其他支出的相对低水平，并通过对被定罪者施以严厉的惩罚来进行补偿。

Of course, if offenders are risk preferrers, the loss in income from offenses is generally minimized by selecting positive and finite values of $p$ and $f$, even though there is no "obvious" offset to a compensated reduction in $p$. One possible offset already hinted at in footnote 27 is that judges or juries may be unwilling to convict offenders if punishments are set very high. Formally, this means that the cost of apprehension and conviction, $C$, would depend not only on $p$ and $O$ but also on $f .{ }^{28}$ If $C$ were more responsive to $f$ than $p$, at least in some regions, [^29] the loss in income could be minimized at finite values of $p$ and $f$ even if offenders were risk avoiders. For then a compensated reduction in $p$ could raise, rather than lower, $C$ and thus contribute to an increase in the loss.
当然，如果违法者是风险偏好者，那么通过选择正的和有限的 $p$ 和 $f$ 值，违法行为造成的收入损失通常会被最小化，即使没有对 $p$ 的补偿性减少的“明显”抵消。脚注27中已经暗示的一个可能的抵消是，如果惩罚设定得非常高，法官或陪审团可能不愿意定罪违法者。正式来说，这意味着逮捕和定罪的成本 $C$ 不仅取决于 $p$ 和 $O$，还取决于 $f$。如果 $C$ 对 $f$ 的反应比对 $p$ 的反应更强，至少在某些区域，[29]即使违法者是风险规避者，收入损失也可以在有限的 $p$ 和 $f$ 值下最小化。因为这样，补偿性减少 $p$ 可能会提高而不是降低 $C$，从而导致损失的增加. 

Risk avoidance might also be consistent with optimal behavior if the loss function were not simply equal to the reduction in income. For example, suppose that the loss were increased by an increase in the ex post "price discrimination" between offenses that are not and those that are cleared by punishment. Then a "compensated" reduction in $p$ would increase the "price discrimination," and the increased loss from this could more than offset the reductions in $C, D$, and $b p f O$.[^30]
风险规避在某些情况下也可能与最佳行为一致，如果损失函数不仅仅等于收入的减少。例如，假设损失因惩罚而未清除的犯罪与已清除的犯罪之间的事后“价格歧视”增加而增加。那么对$p$的“补偿性”减少将增加“价格歧视”，而由此带来的损失增加可能会超过$C, D$和$b p f O$的减少。[^30]

# IV. Shifts in the Behavioral Relations 

This section analyzes the effects of shifts in the basic behavioral relationsthe damage, cost, and supply-of-offenses functions-on the optimal values of $p$ and $f$. Since rigorous proofs can be found in the Mathematical Appendix, here the implications are stressed, and only intuitive proofs are given. The results are used to explain, among other things, why more damaging offenses are punished more severely and more impulsive offenders less severely.
本节分析了基本行为关系的变化——损害、成本和犯罪供给函数——对$p$和$f$的最优值的影响。由于严格的证明可以在数学附录中找到，这里强调的是其含义，并仅提供直观的证明。结果用于解释其他一些问题，例如，为什么造成更大损害的犯罪受到更严厉的惩罚，而冲动型犯罪者则受到较轻的惩罚。

An increase in the marginal damages from a given number of offenses, $D^{\prime}$, increases the marginal cost of changing offenses by a change in either $p$ or $f$ (see Fig. $2 a$ and $b$ ). The optimal number of offenses would necessarily decrease, because the optimal values of both $p$ and $f$ would increase. In this case (and, as shortly seen, in several others), the optimal values of $p$ and $f$ move in the same, rather than in opposite, directions. [^31]
从给定数量的犯罪中边际损害的增加，$D^{\prime}$，会导致通过改变$p$或$f$而改变犯罪的边际成本增加（见图$2 a$和$b$）。最优的犯罪数量必然会减少，因为$p$和$f$的最优值都会增加。在这种情况下（以及稍后看到的几种其他情况中），$p$和$f$的最优值是朝同一方向变化，而不是相反方向变化。[^31]

An interesting application of these conclusions is to different kinds of offenses. Although there are few objective measures of the damages done  by most offenses, it does not take much imagination to conclude that offenses like murder or rape generally do more damage than petty larceny or auto theft. If the other components of the loss in income were the same, the optimal probability of apprehension and conviction and the punishment when convicted would be greater for the more serious offenses.
这些结论的一个有趣应用是针对不同类型的犯罪。尽管对大多数犯罪造成的损害几乎没有客观的衡量标准，但不需要太多想象力就可以得出结论，谋杀或强奸等犯罪通常造成的损害要大于小偷小摸或汽车盗窃。如果收入损失的其他组成部分相同，那么对于更严重的犯罪，最佳的被捕和定罪概率以及定罪后的惩罚将会更高。

If other components of the loss function are ignored, and if actual and optimal probabilities and punishments are positively related, one should find that the more serious felonies have higher probabilities and longer prison terms. And one does: in the table, which lists the felonies in decreasing order of presumed seriousness, both the actual probabilities and the prison terms are positively related to seriousness.
表2展示了美国七种重罪的实际概率和惩罚的一些证据。这些惩罚仅仅是服刑的平均时间，而概率则是估计的定罪人数与估计的犯罪人数之比，毫无疑问包含了较大的误差（参见Smigel，1965年和Ehrlich，1967年的讨论）。如果忽略损失函数的其他组成部分，并且如果实际和最优的概率与惩罚之间存在正相关关系，那么应该会发现更严重的重罪具有更高的概率和更长的监禁期限。实际上确实如此：在表中，重罪按假定的严重性递减排列，实际概率和监禁期限都与严重性呈正相关。

Since an increase in the marginal cost of apprehension and conviction for a given number of offenses, $C^{\prime}$, has identical effects as an increase in marginal damages, it must also reduce the optimal number of offenses and increase the optimal values of $p$ and $f$. On the other hand, an increase in the other component of the cost of apprehension and conviction, $C_{p}$, has no direct effect on the marginal cost of changing offenses with $f$ and reduces the cost of changing offenses with $p$ (see Fig. 3). It therefore reduces the optimal value of $p$ and only partially compensates with an increase in $f$, so that the optimal number of offenses increases. Accordingly, an increase in both $C^{\prime}$ and $C_{p}$ must increase the optimal $f$ but can either increase or decrease the optimal $p$ and optimal number of offenses, depending on the relative importance of the changes in $C^{\prime}$ and $C_{p}$.
由于对给定数量犯罪的逮捕和定罪的边际成本 $C^{\prime}$ 的增加与边际损害的增加具有相同的效果，因此它也必须减少最佳犯罪数量，并增加最佳的 $p$ 和 $f$ 值。另一方面，逮捕和定罪成本的另一个组成部分 $C_{p}$ 的增加对改变 $f$ 的边际成本没有直接影响，并且降低了改变 $p$ 的成本（见图 3）。因此，它减少了最佳 $p$ 的值，并且仅通过增加 $f$ 部分补偿，从而导致最佳犯罪数量增加。因此，$C^{\prime}$ 和 $C_{p}$ 的同时增加必须增加最佳 $f$，但可能会根据 $C^{\prime}$ 和 $C_{p}$ 变化的相对重要性，导致最佳 $p$ 和最佳犯罪数量的增加或减少。

![[Pasted image 20250326011322.png]]

The cost of apprehending and convicting offenders is affected by a variety of forces. An increase in the salaries of policemen increases both $C^{\prime}$ and $C_{p}$, while improved police technology in the form of fingerprinting, ballistic techniques, computer control, and chemical analysis, or police and court "reform" with an emphasis on professionalism and merit, would tend to reduce both, not necessarily by the same extent. Our analysis implies, therefore, that although an improvement in technology and reform may or may not increase the optimal $p$ and reduce the optimal number of offenses, it does reduce the optimal $f$ and thus the need to rely on severe punishments for those convicted. Possibly this explains why the secular improvement in police technology and reform has gone hand in hand with a secular decline in punishments.
逮捕和定罪罪犯的成本受到多种因素的影响。警察工资的增加会同时提高 $C^{\prime}$ 和 $C_{p}$，而指纹识别、弹道技术、计算机控制和化学分析等形式的警务技术改善，或者强调专业性和优才的警察与法院“改革”，则倾向于降低这两者，尽管降低的幅度不一定相同。因此，我们的分析表明，尽管技术和改革的改善可能会增加最佳的 $p$ 并减少最佳的犯罪数量，但它确实降低了最佳的 $f$，从而减少了对重刑的依赖。**这或许可以解释为什么警务技术和改革的世俗改善与惩罚的世俗下降是并行的。**

$C_{p}$, and to a lesser extent $C^{\prime}$, differ significantly between different kinds of offenses. It is easier, for example, to solve a rape or armed robbery than a burglary or auto theft, because the evidence of personal identification is often available in the former and not in the latter offenses. [^32] This might tempt one to argue that the $p$ 's decline significantly as one moves across Table 2 (left to right) primarily because the $C_{p}$ 's are significantly lower for the "personal" felonies listed to the left than for the "impersonal" felonies listed to the right. But this implies that the $f$ 's would increase as one moved across the table, which is patently false. Consequently, the positive correlation between $p, f$, and the severity of offenses observed in the table cannot be explained by a negative correlation between $C_{p}$ (or $C^{\prime}$ ) and severity.
$C_{p}$，以及在较小程度上 $C^{\prime}$，在不同类型的犯罪之间存在显著差异。例如，解决一起强奸或持枪抢劫案件通常比解决一起入室盗窃或汽车盗窃案件要容易，因为前者通常有个人身份的证据，而后者则没有。[^32] 这可能会让人倾向于认为，随着从表2的左侧移动到右侧，$p$ 的下降主要是因为左侧列出的“个人”重罪的 $C_{p}$ 显著低于右侧列出的“非个人”重罪。但这意味着随着在表中移动，$f$ 会增加，这显然是错误的。因此，表中观察到的 $p$、$f$ 和犯罪严重性之间的正相关关系不能通过 $C_{p}$（或 $C^{\prime}$）与严重性之间的负相关关系来解释。

If $b>0$, a reduction in the elasticity of offenses with respect to $f$ increases the marginal revenue of changing offenses by changing $f$ (see Fig. $4 a$ ). The result is an increase in the optimal number of offenses and a decrease in the optimal $f$ that is partially compensated by an increase in the optimal $p$. Similarly, a reduction in the elasticity of offenses with respect to $p$ also increases the optimal number of offenses (see Fig. 4b), decreases the optimal $p$, and partially compensates by an increase in $f$. An equal percentage reduction in both elasticities a fortiori increases the optimal number of offenses and also tends to reduce both $p$ and $f$. If $b=0$, both marginal revenue functions lie along the horizontal axis, and changes in these elasticities have no effect on the optimal values of $p$ and $f$.
如果 $b>0$，减少对 $f$ 的犯罪弹性会通过改变 $f$ 来增加改变犯罪的边际收益（见图 $4 a$）。结果是最佳犯罪数量增加，最佳 $f$ 减少，而最佳 $p$ 的增加部分抵消了这一变化。同样，减少对 $p$ 的犯罪弹性也会增加最佳犯罪数量（见图 4b），减少最佳 $p$，并通过增加 $f$ 部分抵消。如果 $b=0$，两个边际收益函数都位于横轴上，这些弹性的变化对最佳 $p$ 和 $f$ 的值没有影响。

The income of a firm would usually be larger if it could separate, at little cost, its total market into submarkets that have substantially different elasticities of demand: higher prices would be charged in the submarkets having lower elasticities. Similarly, if the total "market" for offenses could be separated into submarkets that differ significantly in the elasticities of supply of offenses, the results above imply that if $b>0$ the total loss would be reduced by "charging" lower "prices"-that is, lower $p$ 's and $f$ 's-in markets with lower elasticities.
如果一家公司能够以较低的成本将其总市场划分为需求弹性差异显著的子市场，那么其收入通常会更高：在需求弹性较低的子市场中会收取更高的价格。同样，如果对犯罪行为的总“市场”能够划分为在犯罪供给弹性上有显著差异的子市场，那么上述结果表明，如果 $b>0$，则通过在弹性较低的市场中“收取”较低的“价格”——即较低的 $p$ 和 $f$——总损失将会减少。

Sometimes it is possible to separate persons committing the same offense into groups that have different responses to punishments. For example, unpremeditated murderers or robbers are supposed to act impulsively and, therefore, to be relatively unresponsive to the size of punishments; likewise, the insane or the young are probably less affected than other offenders by future consequences and, therefore, [^33] probably less deterred by increases in the probability of conviction or in the punishment when convicted. The trend during the twentieth century toward relatively smaller prison terms and greater use of probation and therapy for such groups and, more generally, the trend away from the doctrine of "a given punishment for a given crime" is apparently at least broadly consistent with the implications of the optimality analysis.
有时，可以将犯同样罪行的人分成对惩罚反应不同的群体。例如，非预谋的谋杀者或抢劫犯被认为是冲动行事，因此对惩罚的大小相对不敏感；同样，精神病患者或年轻人可能比其他罪犯更少受到未来后果的影响，因此，可能对定罪概率的增加或被定罪时的惩罚增加反应较小。二十世纪的趋势是对这些群体施加相对较短的监禁时间，并更广泛地使用缓刑和治疗，此外，更普遍地偏离“对特定罪行施以特定惩罚”的原则，这显然至少在广义上与最优性分析的含义是一致的。

An increase in $b$ increases the marginal revenue from changing the number of offenses by changing $p$ or $f$ and thereby increases the optimal number of offenses, reduces the optimal value of $f$, and increases the optimal value of $p$. Some evidence presented in Section II indicates that $b$ is especially large for juveniles in detention homes or adults in prison and is small for fines or adults on parole. The analysis implies, therefore, that other things the same, the optimal $f$ 's would be smaller and the optimal $p$ 's larger if punishment were by one of the former rather than one of the latter methods.
$b$ 的增加会通过改变 $p$ 或 $f$ 来增加改变犯罪数量的边际收益，从而增加最佳犯罪数量，减少最佳 $f$ 的值，并增加最佳 $p$ 的值。在第二节中提供的一些证据表明，对于拘留所中的青少年或监狱中的成年人，$b$ 特别大，而对于罚款或假释中的成年人则较小。因此，分析暗示，如果其他条件相同，采用前者的惩罚方式而不是后者，最佳 $f$ 的值会更小，而最佳 $p$ 的值会更大。

# V. Fines 

## A. Welfare Theorems and Transferable Pricing

The usual optimality conditions in welfare economics depend only on the levels and not on the slopes of marginal cost and average revenue functions, as in the well-known condition that marginal costs equal prices. The social loss from offenses was explicitly introduced as an application of the approach used in welfare economics, and yet slopes as incorporated into elasticities of supply do significantly affect the optimality conditions. Why this difference? The primary explanation would appear to be that it is almost always implicitly assumed that prices paid by consumers are fully transferred to firms and governments, so that there is no social loss from payment.
福利经济学中的通常最优条件仅依赖于水平，而不依赖于边际成本和平均收入函数的斜率，就像著名的边际成本等于价格的条件一样。社会损失的引入是作为福利经济学方法的一种应用，然而，供给的弹性所包含的斜率确实会显著影响最优条件。为什么会有这样的差异？主要的解释似乎是，几乎总是隐含地假设消费者支付的价格完全转移给企业和政府，因此支付并不会造成社会损失。

If there were no social loss from punishments, as with fines, $b$ would equal zero, and the elasticity of supply would drop out of the optimality condition given by equation (21). [^34] If $b>0$, as with imprisonment, some of the payment "by" offenders would not be received by the rest of society, and a net social loss would result. The elasticity of the supply of offenses then becomes an important determinant of the optimality conditions, because it determines the change in social costs caused by a change in punishments.
如果惩罚没有社会损失，比如罚款，$b$ 将等于零，供给的弹性将从方程（21）给出的最优条件中消失。[^34] 如果 $b>0$，如监禁，违法者“支付”的一部分将不会被社会其他成员所接受，从而导致净社会损失。因此，违法行为的供给弹性成为最优条件的重要决定因素，因为它决定了惩罚变化所引起的社会成本变化。

Although transferable monetary pricing is the most common kind today, the other is not unimportant, especially in underdeveloped and Communist countries. Examples in addition to imprisonment and many other punishments are the draft, payments in kind, and queues and other waiting-time forms of rationing that result from legal restrictions on pricing (see Becker, 1965) and from random variations in demand and supply conditions. It is interesting, and deserves further exploration, that the optimality conditions are so significantly affected by a change in the assumptions about the transferability of pricing.
虽然可转让的货币定价是今天最常见的形式，但另一种形式并非不重要，尤其是在欠发达和共产主义国家。除了监禁和许多其他惩罚之外，例子还包括征兵、实物支付，以及由于法律对定价的限制（见Becker，1965）和需求与供给条件的随机变化而导致的排队和其他等待时间形式的配给。有趣的是，且值得进一步探讨的是，最优条件受到关于定价可转让性假设变化的显著影响。

## B. Optimality Conditions 

If $b=0$, say, because punishment was by fine, and if the cost of apprehending and convicting offenders were also zero, the two optimality conditions (21) and (22) would reduce to the same simple condition
如果 $b=0$，比如说，因为惩罚是通过罚款进行的，并且如果抓捕和定罪罪犯的成本也为零，那么两个最优条件（21）和（22）将简化为相同的简单条件。

$$
D^{\prime}(O)=0 \tag{24}
$$

Economists generally conclude that activities causing "external" harm, such as factories that pollute the air or lumber operations that strip the land, should be taxed or otherwise restricted in level until the marginal external harm equalled the marginal private gain, that is, until marginal net damages equalled zero, which is what equation (24) says. If marginal harm always exceeded marginal gain, the optimum level would be presumed to be zero, and that would also be the implication of (24) when suitable inequality conditions were brought in. In other words, if the costs of apprehending, convicting, and punishing offenders were nil and if each offense caused more external harm than private gain, the social loss from offenses would be minimized by setting punishments high enough to eliminate all offenses. Minimizing the social loss would become identical with the criterion of minimizing crime by setting penalties sufficiently high. [^35]
经济学家普遍认为，造成“外部”伤害的活动，例如污染空气的工厂或剥夺土地的伐木作业，应当被征税或以其他方式限制其水平，直到边际外部伤害等于边际私人收益，也就是说，直到边际净损失等于零，这正是方程（24）所表达的。如果边际伤害始终超过边际收益，则最佳水平将被认为是零，而这也是在适当的不等式条件下（24）的含义。换句话说，如果抓捕、定罪和惩罚罪犯的成本为零，并且每次犯罪造成的外部伤害超过私人收益，那么通过设定足够高的惩罚来消除所有犯罪，将使社会损失最小化。最小化社会损失将与通过设定足够高的惩罚来最小化犯罪的标准相同。[^35]

Equation (24) determines the optimal number of offenses, $\hat{O}$, and the fine and probability of conviction must be set at levels that induce offenders to commit just $\hat{O}$ offenses. If the economists' usual theory of choice is applied to illegal activities (see Sec. II), the marginal value of these penalties has to equal the marginal private gain:
方程（24）确定了最佳犯罪数量 $\hat{O}$，罚款和定罪概率必须设定在能够促使犯罪者仅犯下 $\hat{O}$ 次犯罪的水平。如果将经济学家通常的选择理论应用于非法活动（见第二节），那么这些惩罚的边际价值必须等于边际私人收益：

$$
V=G^{\prime}(\hat{O})
$$

where $G^{\prime}(\hat{O})$ is the marginal private gain at $\hat{O}$ and $V$ is the monetary value of the marginal penalties. Since by equations (3) and (24), $D^{\prime}(\hat{O})=$ $H^{\prime}(\hat{O})-G^{\prime}(\hat{O})=0$, one has by substitution in (25)
其中 $G^{\prime}(\hat{O})$ 是在 $\hat{O}$ 处的边际私人收益，$V$ 是边际惩罚的货币价值。由于根据方程 (3) 和 (24)，$D^{\prime}(\hat{O})=$ $H^{\prime}(\hat{O})-G^{\prime}(\hat{O})=0$，因此可以通过在 (25) 中代入得出。

$$
V=H^{\prime}(\hat{O})
$$
The monetary value of the penalties would equal the marginal harm caused by offenses.

Since the cost of apprehension and conviction is assumed equal to zero, the probability of apprehension and conviction could be set equal to unity without cost. The monetary value of penalties would then simply equal the fines imposed, and equation (26) would become
由于逮捕和定罪的成本被假定为零，因此逮捕和定罪的概率可以在没有成本的情况下设定为1。罚款的货币价值将简单等于所施加的罚款，方程（26）将变为

$$
f=H^{\prime}(\hat{O})
$$

Since fines are paid by offenders to the rest of society, a fine determined by (27) would exactly compensate the latter for the marginal harm suffered, and the criterion of minimizing the social loss would be identical, at the margin, with the criterion of compensating "victims." [^36] If the harm to victims always exceeded the gain to offenders, both criteria would reduce in turn to eliminating all offenses.
由于罚款是由违法者支付给社会其他成员的，因此由（27）确定的罚款将恰好补偿社会所遭受的边际损害，而最小化社会损失的标准在边际上将与补偿“受害者”的标准相同。[36] 如果对受害者的损害总是超过对违法者的收益，那么这两个标准最终都将归结为消除所有违法行为。

If the cost of apprehension and conviction were not zero, the optimality condition would have to incorporate marginal costs as well as marginal damages and would become, if the probability of conviction were still assumed to equal unity,
如果逮捕和定罪的成本不是零，最优条件就必须考虑边际成本以及边际损害，并且如果仍然假设定罪的概率等于1，那么条件将变为：

$$
D^{\prime}(\hat{O})+C^{\prime}(\hat{O}, 1)=0
$$

Since $C^{\prime}>0$, (28) requires that $D^{\prime}<0$ or that the marginal private gain exceed the marginal external harm, which generally means a smaller number of offenses than when $D^{\prime}=0 .{ }^{37}$ It is easy to show that equation (28) would be satisfied if the fine equalled the sum of marginal harm and marginal costs:
由于 $C^{\prime}>0$，(28) 要求 $D^{\prime}<0$ 或者边际私人收益超过边际外部损害，这通常意味着比 $D^{\prime}=0$ 时的犯罪数量要少。很容易证明，如果罚款等于边际损害和边际成本之和，则方程 (28) 将得到满足：

$$
f=H^{\prime}(\hat{O})+C^{\prime}(\hat{O}, 1) .^{38} \tag{29}
$$

In other words, offenders have to compensate for the cost of catching them as well as for the harm they directly do, which is a natural generalization of the usual externality analysis.
换句话说，违法者必须赔偿抓捕他们的成本以及他们直接造成的损害，这是一种对通常外部性分析的自然概括。

The optimality condition

$$
D^{\prime}(\hat{O})+C^{\prime}(\hat{O}, \hat{p})+C_{p}(\hat{O}, \hat{p}) \frac{1}{\hat{O}_{p}}=0 \tag{30}
$$

would replace equation (28) if the fine rather than the probability of conviction were fixed. Equation (30) would usually imply that $D^{\prime}(\tilde{O})>0,{ }^{39}$ and thus that the number of offenses would exceed the optimal number when costs were zero. Whether costs of apprehension and conviction increase or decrease the optimal number of offenses largely depends, therefore, on whether penalties are changed by a change in the fine or in the probability of conviction. Of course, if both are subject to control, the optimal probability of conviction would be arbitrarily close to zero, unless the social loss function differed from equation (18) (see the discussion in Sec. III).
最优性条件将替代方程（28），如果罚款而不是定罪的概率被固定。方程（30）通常意味着 $D^{\prime}(\tilde{O})>0,{ }^{39}$，因此在成本为零时，犯罪数量将超过最优数量。因此，逮捕和定罪的成本是增加还是减少最优犯罪数量在很大程度上取决于罚款或定罪概率的变化是否会改变处罚。当然，如果两者都受到控制，最优的定罪概率将会非常接近于零，除非社会损失函数与方程（18）不同（参见第III节的讨论）。

## C. The Case for Fines 

Just as the probability of conviction and the severity of punishment are subject to control by society, so too is the form of punishment: legislation usually specifies whether an offense is punishable by fines, probation, institutionalization, or some combination. Is it merely an accident, or have optimality considerations determined that today, in most countries, fines are the predominant form of punishment, with institutionalization reserved for the more serious offenses? This section presents several arguments which imply that social welfare is increased if fines are used whenever feasible.
正如定罪的可能性和惩罚的严重性受到社会的控制，惩罚的形式也是如此：立法通常规定某种罪行是否可处以罚款、缓刑、监禁或某种组合。这仅仅是偶然，还是最优考虑决定了今天在大多数国家，罚款是主要的惩罚形式，而监禁则保留给更严重的罪行？本节提出几个论点，暗示如果在可行的情况下使用罚款，社会福利将会增加。

In the first place, probation and institutionalization use up social resources, and fines do not, since the latter are basically just transfer payments, while the former use resources in the form of guards, supervisory personnel, probation officers, and the offenders' own time. [^40] Table 1 indicates that the cost is not minor either: in the United States in 1965, about $\$ 1$ billion was spent on "correction," and this estimate excludes, of course, the value of the loss in offenders' time. [^41]
首先，缓刑和监禁消耗了社会资源，而罚款则没有，因为后者基本上只是转移支付，而前者则以警卫、监督人员、缓刑官和罪犯自身的时间的形式使用资源。[40] 表1显示，成本也并不小：在1965年的美国，约花费了10亿美元用于“矫正”，当然，这一估计并未包括罪犯时间损失的价值。[41]

$$
\frac{d C}{d p}=C^{\prime} \frac{\partial O}{\partial p}+C_{p}>0
$$

then

$$
C^{\prime}+C_{p} \frac{1}{\partial O / \partial p}<0
$$

and

$$
D^{\prime}(\tilde{O})=-\left(C^{\prime}+C_{p} \frac{1}{\partial O / \partial p}\right)>0
$$

Moreover, the determination of the optimal number of offenses and severity of punishments is somewhat simplified by the use of fines. A wise use of fines requires knowledge of marginal gains and harm and of marginal apprehension and conviction costs; admittedly, such knowledge is not easily acquired. A wise use of imprisonment and other punishments must know this too, however, and, in addition, must know about the elasticities of response of offenses to changes in punishments. As the bitter controversies over the abolition of capital punishment suggest, it has been difficult to learn about these elasticities.
此外，确定最佳的犯罪数量和惩罚的严重程度在一定程度上通过罚款的使用得到了简化。明智地使用罚款需要了解边际收益和损害，以及边际逮捕和定罪成本；诚然，这种知识并不容易获得。明智地使用监禁和其他惩罚也必须了解这些，同时，还必须了解犯罪对惩罚变化的反应弹性。正如关于废除死刑的激烈争论所暗示的那样，了解这些弹性是非常困难的。

I suggested earlier that premeditation, sanity, and age can enter into the determination of punishments as proxies for the elasticities of response. These characteristics may not have to be considered in levying fines, because the optimal fines, as determined, say, by equations (27) or (29), do not depend on elasticities. Perhaps this partly explains why economists discussing externalities almost never mention motivation or intent, while sociologists and lawyers discussing criminal behavior invariably do. The former assume that punishment is by a monetary tax or fine, while the latter assume that non-monetary punishments are used.
我之前提到，预谋、理智和年龄可以作为反应弹性的代理因素，影响惩罚的决定。这些特征在征收罚款时可能不需要考虑，因为最佳罚款，如方程（27）或（29）所确定的，并不依赖于弹性。也许这部分解释了为什么经济学家在讨论外部性时几乎从不提及动机或意图，而社会学家和律师在讨论犯罪行为时则总是提到。前者假设惩罚是通过货币税或罚款进行的，而后者则假设使用的是非货币惩罚。

Fines provide compensation to victims, and optimal fines at the margin fully compensate victims and restore the status quo ante, so that they are no worse off than if offenses were not committed. [^42] Not only do other punishments fail to compensate, but they also require "victims" to spend additional resources in carrying out the punishment. It is not surprising, therefore, that the anger and fear felt toward ex-convicts who in fact have not " paid their debt to society" have resulted in additional punishments, [^43] including legal restrictions on their political and economic opportunities [^44] and informal restrictions on their social acceptance. Moreover, the absence of compensation encourages efforts to change and otherwise "rehabilitate" offenders through psychiatric counseling, therapy, and other programs. Since fines do compensate and do not create much additional cost, anger toward and fear of appropriately fined persons do not easily develop. As a result, additional punishments are not usually levied against "ex-finees," nor are strong efforts made to "rehabilitate" them.
罚款为受害者提供了赔偿，而边际上的最佳罚款能够充分补偿受害者并恢复到原状，使他们的处境不比未发生犯罪时更糟。因此，其他惩罚不仅无法提供补偿，还要求“受害者”在执行惩罚时花费额外的资源。因此，针对那些实际上并未“偿还社会债务”的前罪犯所感受到的愤怒和恐惧导致了额外的惩罚，包括对他们政治和经济机会的法律限制以及对他们社会接受度的非正式限制。此外，缺乏赔偿鼓励通过精神咨询、治疗和其他项目来改变和“ rehabilitate”罪犯。由于罚款确实提供了补偿且不会产生太多额外成本，因此对适当罚款的人的愤怒和恐惧并不容易产生。因此，通常不会对“前罚款者”施加额外的惩罚，也不会对他们进行强有力的“ rehabilitate”努力。

One argument made against fines is that they are immoral because, in effect, they permit offenses to be bought for a price in the same way that bread or other goods are bought for a price. [^45] A fine can be considered the price of an offense, but so too can any other form of punishment; for example, the "price" of stealing a car might be six months in jail. The only difference is in the units of measurement: fines are prices measured in monetary units, imprisonments are prices measured in time units, etc. If anything, monetary units are to be preferred here as they are generally preferred in pricing and accounting.
对罚款的一个反对论点是，它们是不道德的，因为实际上，它们允许以价格购买违法行为，就像购买面包或其他商品一样。[^45] 罚款可以被视为违法行为的价格，但任何其他形式的惩罚也可以如此；例如，偷一辆车的“价格”可能是六个月的监禁。唯一的区别在于计量单位：罚款是以货币单位计量的价格，监禁是以时间单位计量的价格，等等。如果说有什么不同的话，货币单位在这里更受欢迎，因为它们在定价和会计中通常更受青睐。

Optimal fines determined from equation (29) depend only on the marginal harm and cost and not at all on the economic positions of offenders. This has been criticized as unfair, and fines proportional to the incomes of offenders have been suggested. [^46] If the goal is to minimize the social loss in income from offenses, and not to take vengeance or to inflict harm on offenders, then fines should depend on the total harm done by offenders, and not directly on their income, race, sex, etc. In the same way, the monetary value of optimal prison sentences and other punishments depends on the harm, costs, and elasticities of response, but not directly on an offender's income. Indeed, if the monetary value of the punishment by, say, imprisonment were independent of income, the length of the sentence would be inversely related to income, because the value placed on a given sentence is positively related to income.
最佳罚款由方程（29）确定，仅依赖于边际损害和成本，而与违法者的经济状况无关。这一点受到批评，被认为是不公平的，因此有人建议罚款应与违法者的收入成比例。[46] 如果目标是最小化因犯罪造成的社会收入损失，而不是报复或对违法者施加伤害，那么罚款应依赖于违法者造成的总损害，而不是直接与他们的收入、种族、性别等相关。同样，最佳监禁刑期和其他惩罚的货币价值也依赖于损害、成本和反应的弹性，而不是直接与违法者的收入相关。实际上，如果惩罚的货币价值，比如监禁，与收入无关，那么刑期的长短将与收入成反比，因为对特定刑期的价值评估与收入呈正相关。

We might detour briefly to point out some interesting implications for the probability of conviction of the fact that the monetary value of a given fine is obviously the same for all offenders, while the monetary equivalent or "value" of a given prison sentence or probation period is generally positively related to an offender's income. The discussion in Section II suggested that actual probabilities of conviction are not fixed to all offenders but usually vary with their age, sex, race, and, in particular, income. Offenders with higher earnings have an incentive to spend more on planning their offenses, on good lawyers, on legal appeals, and even on bribery to reduce the probability of apprehension and conviction for offenses punishable by, say, a given prison term, because the cost to them of conviction is relatively large compared to the cost of these expenditures.
我们可能会稍作绕行，指出一些有趣的含义，即对于所有罪犯而言，罚款的货币价值显然是相同的，而特定监禁刑期或缓刑期的货币等价物或“价值”通常与罪犯的收入呈正相关。第二节的讨论表明，实际的定罪概率并不是固定的，而是通常随着罪犯的年龄、性别、种族，尤其是收入而变化。收入较高的罪犯有动力在策划犯罪、聘请优秀律师、进行法律上诉，甚至贿赂上花费更多，以降低被抓捕和定罪的概率，因为与这些支出相比，定罪对他们的成本相对较高。

Similarly, however, poorer offenders have an incentive to use more of their time in planning their offenses, in court appearances, and the like to reduce the probability of conviction for offenses punishable by a given fine, because the cost to them of conviction is relatively large compared to the value of their time. [^47] The implication is that the probability of conviction would be systematically related to the earnings of offenders: negatively for offenses punishable by imprisonment and positively for those punishable by fines. Although a negative relation for felonies and other offenses punishable by imprisonment has been frequently observed and deplored (see President's Commission, 1967c, pp. 139-53), I do not know of any studies of the relation for fines or of any recognition that the observed negative relation may be more a consequence of the nature of the punishment than of the influence of wealth.
同样，经济条件较差的罪犯有动力花更多时间来策划他们的犯罪、出庭等，以降低因可处以特定罚款的罪行而被定罪的概率，因为对他们来说，被定罪的成本相对较高，远超过他们时间的价值。[47] **这意味着，定罪的概率与罪犯的收入之间会存在系统性的关系：对于可处以监禁的罪行呈负相关，而对于可处以罚款的罪行则呈正相关。** 尽管对于重罪和其他可处以监禁的罪行，负相关的现象经常被观察到并受到谴责（见总统委员会，1967年，页139-153），但我不知道有任何关于罚款的关系的研究，或者对观察到的负相关可能更多是惩罚性质的结果而非财富影响的认识。

Another argument made against fines is that certain crimes, like murder or rape, are so heinous that no amount of money could compensate for the harm inflicted. This argument has obvious merit and is a special case of the more general principle that fines cannot be relied on exclusively whenever the harm exceeds the resources of offenders. For then victims could not be fully compensated by offenders, and fines would have to be supplemented with prison terms or other punishments in order to discourage offenses optimally. This explains why imprisonments, probation, and parole are major punishments for the more serious felonies; considerable harm is inflicted, and felonious offenders lack sufficient resources to compensate. Since fines are preferable, it also suggests the need for a flexible system of instalment fines to enable offenders to pay fines more readily and thus avoid other punishments.
另一个反对罚款的论点是，某些罪行，如谋杀或强奸，是如此可怕，以至于任何金额的金钱都无法弥补造成的伤害。这个论点显然是有道理的，并且是一个更一般原则的特殊情况，即当伤害超过罪犯的资源时，罚款不能被单独依赖。因为这样，受害者无法得到罪犯的充分赔偿，罚款必须与监禁或其他惩罚相结合，以便最佳地威慑犯罪。这解释了为什么监禁、缓刑和假释是对更严重罪犯的主要惩罚；造成了相当大的伤害，而犯罪者缺乏足够的资源来进行赔偿。由于罚款是更可取的，这也表明需要一个灵活的分期罚款制度，以便让罪犯更容易支付罚款，从而避免其他惩罚。

This analysis implies that if some offenders could pay the fine for a given offense and others could not, [^48] the former should be punished solely by fine and the latter partly by other methods. In essence, therefore, these methods become a vehicle for punishing "debtors" to society. Before the cry is raised that the system is unfair, especially to poor offenders, consider the following.
这项分析暗示，如果某些违法者能够为特定罪行支付罚款，而其他人则无法支付，[^48] 前者应仅受到罚款的惩罚，而后者则应部分通过其他方式受到惩罚。因此，从本质上讲，这些方法成为惩罚社会“债务人”的工具。在有人呼吁该系统不公，尤其是对贫困违法者不公之前，请考虑以下几点。

Those punished would be debtors in "transactions" that were never agreed to by their "creditors," not in voluntary transactions, such as loans, [^49] for which suitable precautions could be taken in advance by creditors. Moreover, punishment in any economic system based on voluntary market transactions inevitably must distinguish between such "debtors" and others. If a rich man purchases a car and a poor man steals one, the former is congratulated, while the latter is often sent to prison when apprehended. Yet the rich man's purchase is equivalent to a "theft" subsequently compensated by a "fine" equal to the price of the car, while the poor man, in effect, goes to prison because he cannot pay this "fine."
那些受到惩罚的人是那些在“交易”中欠债的债务人，而这些交易并未得到他们的“债权人”的同意，而不是自愿的交易，例如贷款，[^49] 对于这种交易，债权人可以提前采取适当的预防措施。此外，在任何基于自愿市场交易的经济体系中，惩罚必然要区分这种“债务人”和其他人。如果一个富人购买了一辆车，而一个穷人偷了一辆，前者会受到祝贺，而后者在被抓住时往往会被送进监狱。然而，富人的购买实际上相当于一种“盗窃”，随后通过与汽车价格相等的“罚款”进行补偿，而穷人实际上是因为无法支付这笔“罚款”而入狱。

Whether a punishment like imprisonment in lieu of a full fine for offenders lacking sufficient resources is "fair" depends, of course, on the length of the prison term compared to the fine. [^50] For example, a prison term of one week in lieu of a $\$ 10,000$ fine would, if anything, be "unfair" to wealthy offenders paying the fine. Since imprisonment is a more costly punishment to society than fines, the loss from offenses would be reduced by a policy of leniency toward persons who are imprisoned because they cannot pay fines. Consequently, optimal prison terms for "debtors" would not be "unfair" to them in the sense that the monetary equivalent to them of the prison terms would be less than the value of optimal fines, which in turn would equal the harm caused or the "debt." [^51]
对于缺乏足够资源的违法者来说，监禁作为替代全额罚款的惩罚是否“公平”，当然取决于监禁期限与罚款的比较。[^50] 例如，若一周的监禁替代$10,000的罚款，对支付罚款的富有违法者来说，反而会显得“不公平”。由于监禁对社会来说是一种比罚款更昂贵的惩罚，因此，针对那些因无法支付罚款而被监禁的人的宽容政策将减少因违法行为造成的损失。因此，对于“债务人”而言，最佳的监禁期限在某种意义上并不会“对他们不公平”，因为对他们而言，监禁期限的货币等价物将低于最佳罚款的价值，而最佳罚款又等于造成的损害或“债务”。

It appears, however, that "debtors" are often imprisoned at rates of exchange with fines that place a low value on time in prison. Although I have not seen systematic evidence on the different punishments actually offered convicted offenders, and the choices they made, many statutes in the United States do permit fines and imprisonment that place a low value on time in prison. For example, in New York State, Class A Misdemeanors can be punished by a prison term as long as one year or a fine no larger than $\$ 1,000$ and Class B Misdemeanors, by a term as long as three months or a fine no larger than $\$ 500$ (Laws of New York, 1965, chap. 1030, Arts. 70 and 80). [^52] According to my analysis, these statutes permit excessive prison sentences relative to the fines, which may explain why imprisonment in lieu of fines is considered unfair to poor offenders, who often must "choose" the prison alternative.
然而，似乎“债务人”常常以低估监禁时间价值的罚款汇率被监禁。尽管我没有看到关于被定罪的罪犯所实际提供的不同惩罚及其选择的系统性证据，但美国的许多法律确实允许罚款和监禁，这些罚款和监禁对监禁时间的价值评估较低。例如，在纽约州，A级轻罪的惩罚可以是最长一年监禁或不超过$1,000的罚款，而B级轻罪则可以是最长三个月的监禁或不超过$500的罚款（纽约法律，1965年，第1030章，第70和80条）。根据我的分析，这些法律允许相对于罚款而言过长的监禁刑期，这可能解释了为什么以监禁替代罚款被认为对贫困罪犯不公平，因为他们常常必须“选择”监禁这一替代方案。

## D. Compensation and the Criminal Law 

Actual criminal proceedings in the United States appear to seek a mixture of deterrence, compensation, and vengeance. I have already indicated that these goals are somewhat contradictory and cannot generally be simultaneously achieved; for example, if punishment were by fine, minimizing the social loss from offenses would be equivalent to compensating "victims" fully, and deterrence or vengeance could only be partially pursued. Therefore, if the case for fines were accepted, and punishment by optimal fines became the norm, the traditional approach to criminal law would have to be significantly modified.
在美国，实际的刑事诉讼似乎寻求威慑、赔偿和报复的混合。我已经指出，这些目标在某种程度上是矛盾的，通常无法同时实现；例如，如果惩罚是罚款，那么最小化犯罪带来的社会损失就等同于对“受害者”进行充分赔偿，而威慑或报复只能部分追求。因此，如果接受罚款的理由，并且以最佳罚款作为惩罚的常态，那么对刑法的传统方法将必须进行重大修改。

First and foremost, the primary aim of all legal proceedings would become the same: not punishment or deterrence, but simply the assessment of the "harm" done by defendants. Much of traditional criminal law would become a branch of the law of torts, [^53] say "social torts," in which the public would collectively sue for "public" harm. A "criminal" action would be defined fundamentally not by the nature of the action [^54] but by the inability of a person to compensate for the "harm" that he caused. Thus an action would be "criminal" precisely because it results in uncompensated "harm" to others. Criminal law would cover all such actions, while tort law would cover all other (civil) actions.
首先，所有法律程序的主要目标将变为相同：不是惩罚或威慑，而是简单地评估被告造成的“伤害”。传统刑法的许多内容将成为侵权法的一个分支，[53] 可以称之为“社会侵权”，在这种情况下，公众将集体起诉以追求“公共”伤害。“刑事”行为的定义将根本上不是基于行为的性质，[54] 而是基于一个人无法补偿他造成的“伤害”。因此，一个行为之所以被视为“刑事”，正是因为它导致了对他人的未补偿的“伤害”。刑法将涵盖所有此类行为，而侵权法将涵盖所有其他（民事）行为。

As a practical example of the fundamental changes that would be wrought, consider the antitrust field. Inspired in part by the economist's classic demonstration that monopolies distort the allocation of resources and reduce economic welfare, the United States has outlawed conspiracies and other constraints of trade. In practice, defendants are often simply required to cease the objectionable activity, although sometimes they are also fined, become subject to damage suits, or are jailed.
作为一个实际例子，考虑反垄断领域所带来的根本变化。部分受到经济学家经典论证的启发，即垄断扭曲了资源的配置并降低了经济福利，美国已经禁止了阴谋和其他贸易限制。在实践中，被告通常只需停止有争议的活动，尽管有时他们还会被罚款、面临损害赔偿诉讼或被监禁。

If compensation were stressed, the main purpose of legal proceedings would be to levy fines equal to [^55] the harm inflicted on society by constraints of trade. There would be no point to cease and desist orders, imprisonment, ridicule, or dissolution of companies. If the economist's theory about monopoly is correct, and if optimal fines were levied, firms would automatically cease any constraints of trade, because the gain to them would be less than the harm they cause and thus less than the fines expected. On the other hand, if Schumpeter and other critics are correct, and certain constraints of trade raise the level of economic welfare, fines could fully compensate society for the harm done, and yet some constraints would not cease, because the gain to participants would exceed the harm to others. [^56]
如果强调赔偿，法律程序的主要目的将是对因贸易限制而对社会造成的伤害征收相等的罚款[^55]。停止和 desist 命令、监禁、嘲笑或公司解散将毫无意义。如果经济学家关于垄断的理论是正确的，并且如果征收了最佳罚款，企业将自动停止任何贸易限制，因为对它们的收益将低于它们造成的伤害，因此也低于预期的罚款。另一方面，如果熊彼特和其他批评者是正确的，某些贸易限制提高了经济福利水平，罚款可以完全补偿社会所遭受的伤害，但某些限制仍然不会停止，因为参与者的收益将超过对他人的伤害。[ ^56]

One unexpected advantage, therefore, from stressing compensation and fines rather than punishment and deterrence is that the validity of the classical position need not be judged a priori. If valid, compensating fines would discourage all constraints of trade and would achieve the classical aims. If not, such fines would permit the socially desirable constraints to continue and, at the same time, would compensate society for the harm done.
因此，强调赔偿和罚款而非惩罚和威慑的一个意想不到的好处是，经典立场的有效性不必事先判断。如果有效，赔偿性罚款将会抑制所有贸易限制，并实现经典目标。如果无效，这些罚款将允许社会所期望的限制继续存在，同时也会补偿社会所遭受的损害。

Of course, as participants in triple-damage suits are well aware, the harm done is not easily measured, and serious mistakes would be inevitable. However, it is also extremely difficult to measure the harm in many civil suits, [^57] yet these continue to function, probably reasonably well on the whole. Moreover, as experience accumulated, the margin of error would decline, and rules of thumb would develop. Finally, one must realize that difficult judgments are also required by the present antitrust policy, such as deciding that certain industries are "workably" competitive or that certain mergers reduce competition. An emphasis on fines and compensation would at least help avoid irrelevant issues by focusing attention on the information most needed for intelligent social policy.
当然，参与三重损害诉讼的各方都清楚，造成的损害并不容易衡量，严重的错误是不可避免的。然而，在许多民事诉讼中，衡量损害同样极为困难，尽管如此，这些诉讼仍然在继续运作，整体上可能相当顺利。此外，随着经验的积累，误差范围会减少，经验法则也会逐渐形成。最后，人们必须意识到，目前的反垄断政策同样需要做出困难的判断，例如决定某些行业是否“可行性”竞争，或某些合并是否会减少竞争。强调罚款和赔偿至少可以通过将注意力集中在制定智能社会政策所需的信息上，帮助避免无关的问题。

# VII. Some Applications 

## A. Optimal Benefits

Our analysis of crime is a generalization of the economist's analysis of external harm or diseconomies. Analytically, the generalization consists in introducing costs of apprehension and conviction, which make the probability of apprehension and conviction an important decision variable, and in treating punishment by imprisonment and other methods as well as by monetary payments. A crime is apparently not so different analytically from any other activity that produces external harm and when crimes are punishable by fines, the analytical differences virtually vanish.
我们对犯罪的分析是对经济学家对外部伤害或不经济现象分析的概括。从分析上看，这一概括在于引入逮捕和定罪的成本，使得逮捕和定罪的概率成为一个重要的决策变量，并将监禁及其他方法的惩罚与金钱支付一起考虑。显然，从分析的角度来看，犯罪与任何其他产生外部伤害的活动并没有太大区别，当犯罪受到罚款惩罚时，分析上的差异几乎消失。

Discussions of external economies or advantages are usually perfectly symmetrical to those of diseconomies, yet one searches in vain for analogues to the law of torts and criminality. Generally, compensation cannot be collected for the external advantages as opposed to harm caused, and no public officials comparable to policemen and district attorneys apprehend and "convict" benefactors rather than offenders. Of course, there is public interest in benefactors: medals, prizes, titles, and other privileges have been awarded to military heroes, government officials, scientists, scholars, artists, and businessmen by public and private bodies. Among the most famous are Nobel Prizes, Lenin Prizes, the Congressional Medal of Honor, knighthood, and patent rights. But these are piecemeal efforts that touch a tiny fraction of the population and lack the guidance of any body of law that codifies and analyzes different kinds of advantages.
外部经济或优势的讨论通常与不经济的讨论完全对称，然而人们却徒劳地寻找与侵权法和犯罪法相类似的内容。一般来说，无法就外部优势获得赔偿，而不是因造成的伤害，且没有与警察和地区检察官相当的公共官员来逮捕和“定罪”恩人而非罪犯。当然，社会对恩人是有公共利益的：军事英雄、政府官员、科学家、学者、艺术家和商人曾获得公共和私人机构颁发的奖章、奖项、头衔和其他特权。其中最著名的包括诺贝尔奖、列宁奖、国会荣誉勋章、骑士称号和专利权。但这些都是零散的努力，仅触及极小一部分人口，缺乏任何法律体系的指导来规范和分析不同种类的优势。

Possibly the explanation for this lacuna is that criminal and tort law developed at the time when external harm was more common than advantages, or possibly the latter have been difficult to measure and thus considered too prone to favoritism. In any case, it is clear that the asymmetry in the law does not result from any analytical asymmetry, for a formal analysis of advantages, benefits, and benefactors can be developed that is quite symmetrical to the analysis of damages, offenses, and offenders. A function $A(B)$, for example, can give the net social advantages from $B$ benefits in the same way that $D(O)$ gives the net damages from $O$ offenses. Likewise, $K\left(B, p_{1}\right)$ can give the cost of apprehending and rewarding benefactors, where $p_{1}$ is the probability of so doing, with $K^{\prime}$ and $K_{p}>0 ; B\left(p_{1}, a, v\right)$ can give the supply of benefits, where $a$ is the award per benefit and $v$ represents other determinants, with $\partial B / \partial p_{1}$ and $\partial B / \partial a>0$; and $b_{1}$ can be the fraction of $a$ that is a net loss to society. Instead of a loss function showing the decrease in social income from offenses, there can be a profit function showing the increase in income from benefits:
可能造成这一空白的解释是，刑法和侵权法的发展发生在外部伤害比利益更为普遍的时期，或者后者可能难以衡量，因此被认为过于容易受到偏袒。无论如何，法律中的这种不对称显然并不是由于任何分析上的不对称，因为可以发展出一个关于利益、好处和恩人的正式分析，这与对损害、犯罪和犯罪者的分析是相当对称的。例如，函数 $A(B)$ 可以给出从 $B$ 好处中获得的净社会利益，正如 $D(O)$ 给出从 $O$ 犯罪中产生的净损害一样。同样，$K\left(B, p_{1}\right)$ 可以给出抓捕和奖励恩人的成本，其中 $p_{1}$ 是这样做的概率，且 $K^{\prime}$ 和 $K_{p}>0$；$B\left(p_{1}, a, v\right)$ 可以给出好处的供给，其中 $a$ 是每个好处的奖励，$v$ 代表其他决定因素，且 $\partial B / \partial p_{1}$ 和 $\partial B / \partial a>0$；$b_{1}$ 可以是 $a$ 中对社会的净损失的部分。与显示因犯罪导致社会收入减少的损失函数相反，可以有一个显示因好处导致收入增加的利润函数：

$$
\Pi=A(B)-K\left(B, p_{1}\right)-b_{1} p_{1} a B
$$

If $\Pi$ is maximized by choosing appropriate values of $p_{1}$ and $a$, the optimality conditions analogous to equations (21) and (22) are
如果通过选择适当的 $p_{1}$ 和 $a$ 来最大化 $\Pi$，那么与方程 (21) 和 (22) 类似的最优条件为

$$
A^{\prime}-K^{\prime}=b_{1} p_{1} a\left(1+\frac{1}{e_{a}}\right)
$$

and

$$
A^{\prime}-K^{\prime}-K_{p} \frac{\partial p_{1}}{\partial B}=b_{1} p_{1} a\left(1+\frac{1}{e_{p}}\right)
$$

where

$$
e_{a}=\frac{\partial B}{\partial a} \frac{a}{B}
$$

and

$$
e_{p}=\frac{\partial B}{\partial p_{1}} \frac{p_{1}}{B}
$$

are both greater than zero. The implications of these equations are related to and yet differ in some important respects from those discussed earlier for (21) and (22).
这两个数都大于零。这些方程的含义与之前讨论的（21）和（22）在某些重要方面相关，但又有所不同。

For example, if $b_{1}>0$, which means that $a$ is not a pure transfer but costs society resources, clearly (34) and (35) imply that $e_{p}>e_{a}$, since both $K_{p}>0$ and $\partial p_{1} / \partial B>0$. This is analogous to the implication of (21) and (22) that $\varepsilon_{p}>\varepsilon_{f}$, but, while the latter implies that, at the margin, offenders are risk preferrers, the former implies that, at the margin, benefactors are risk avoiders. [^61] Thus, while the optimal values of $p$ and $f$ would be in a region where "crime does not pay"-in the sense that the marginal income of criminals would be less than that available to them in less risky legal activities-the optimal values of $p_{1}$ and $a$ would be where "benefits do pay"-in the same sense that the marginal income of benefactors would exceed that available to them in less risky activities. In this sense it "pays" to do "good" and does not "pay" to do "bad."
例如，如果 $b_{1}>0$，这意味着 $a$ 不是纯粹的转移，而是消耗了社会资源，显然 (34) 和 (35) 暗示 $e_{p}>e_{a}$，因为 $K_{p}>0$ 和 $\partial p_{1} / \partial B>0$。这类似于 (21) 和 (22) 的暗示，即 $\varepsilon_{p}>\varepsilon_{f}$，但后者暗示在边际上，违法者是风险偏好者，而前者则暗示在边际上，施惠者是风险规避者。[^61] 因此，尽管 $p$ 和 $f$ 的最优值将位于“犯罪不赚钱”的区域——在这个意义上，犯罪者的边际收入将低于他们在风险较低的合法活动中可获得的收入——但 $p_{1}$ 和 $a$ 的最优值将位于“利益确实赚钱”的地方——在同样的意义上，施惠者的边际收入将超过他们在风险较低的活动中可获得的收入。从这个意义上说，做“好事”是“划算的”，而做“坏事”则是不“划算的”。

As an illustration of the analysis, consider the problem of rewarding inventors for their inventions. The function $A(B)$ gives the total social value of $B$ inventions, and $A^{\prime}$ gives the marginal value of an additional one. The function $K\left(B, p_{1}\right)$ gives the cost of finding and rewarding inventors; if a patent system is used, it measures the cost of a patent office, of preparing applications, and of the lawyers, judges, and others involved in patent litigation. [^62] The elasticities $e_{p}$ and $e_{a}$ measure the response of inventors to changes in the probability and magnitude of awards, while $b_{1}$ measures the social cost of the method used to award inventors. With a patent system, the cost consists in a less extensive use of an invention than would otherwise occur, and in any monopoly power so created.
作为分析的一个例子，考虑奖励发明者其发明的问题。函数 $A(B)$ 表示 $B$ 项发明的总社会价值，而 $A^{\prime}$ 表示额外一项发明的边际价值。函数 $K\left(B, p_{1}\right)$ 表示寻找和奖励发明者的成本；如果使用专利制度，它衡量的是专利局的成本、准备申请的成本，以及参与专利诉讼的律师、法官和其他相关人员的成本。[ ^62 ] 弹性 $e_{p}$ 和 $e_{a}$ 衡量发明者对奖励概率和金额变化的反应，而 $b_{1}$ 衡量用于奖励发明者的方法的社会成本。在专利制度下，成本体现在对发明的使用不如本来可能的那样广泛，以及由此产生的任何垄断权力。

Equations (34) and (35) imply that with any system having $b_{1}>0$, the smaller the elasticities of response of inventors, the smaller should be the probability and magnitude of awards. (The value of a patent can be changed, for example, by changing its life.) This shows the relevance of the controversy between those who maintain that most inventions stem from a basic desire "to know" and those who maintain that most stem from the prospects of financial awards, especially today with the emphasis on systematic investment in research and development. The former quite consistently usually advocate a weak patent system, while the latter equally consistently advocate its strengthening.
方程（34）和（35）表明，对于任何具有 $b_{1}>0$ 的系统，发明者的响应弹性越小，奖励的概率和幅度就应该越小。（例如，通过改变专利的有效期，可以改变专利的价值。）这显示了关于大多数发明源于“求知”基本欲望与大多数发明源于财务奖励前景之间争论的相关性，尤其是在今天强调对研究和开发进行系统投资的背景下。前者通常一致主张弱专利制度，而后者同样一致主张加强专利制度。

Even if $A^{\prime}$, the marginal value of an invention, were "sizeable," the optimal decision would be to abolish property rights in an invention, that is, to set $p_{1}=0$, if $b_{1}$ and $K^{63}$ were sufficiently large and/or the elasticities $e_{p}$ and $e_{a}$ sufficiently small. Indeed, practically all arguments to eliminate or greatly alter the patent system have been based either on its alleged costliness, large $K$ or $b_{1}$, or lack of effectiveness, low $e_{p}$ or $e_{a}$ (see, for example, Plant, 1934, or Arrow, 1962).
即使 $A^{\prime}$，一种发明的边际价值，"相当可观"，最佳决策仍然是废除对发明的产权，即设定 $p_{1}=0$，如果 $b_{1}$ 和 $K^{63}$ 足够大，和/或弹性 $e_{p}$ 和 $e_{a}$ 足够小。实际上，几乎所有主张消除或大幅改变专利制度的论点都基于其所谓的高成本、大 $K$ 或 $b_{1}$，或缺乏有效性、低 $e_{p}$ 或 $e_{a}$（例如，参见 Plant, 1934，或 Arrow, 1962）。

If a patent system were replaced by a system of cash prizes, the elasticities of response would become irrelevant for the determination of optimal policies, because $b_{1}$ would then be approximately zero. [^64] A system of prizes would, moreover, have many of the same other advantages that fines have in punishing offenders (see the discussion in Sec. V). One significant advantage of a patent system, however, is that it automatically "meters" $A^{\prime}$, that is, provides an award that is automatically positively related to $A^{\prime}$, while a system of prizes (or of fines and imprisonment) has to estimate $A^{\prime}$ (or $D^{\prime}$ ) independently and often somewhat arbitrarily.
如果专利制度被现金奖励制度取代，响应的弹性将对最佳政策的确定变得无关紧要，因为 $b_{1}$ 将大约为零。[ ^64 ] 此外，奖励制度还将具有许多与罚款在惩罚违法者时相同的其他优点（见第 V 节的讨论）。然而，专利制度的一个显著优点是它自动“计量” $A^{\prime}$，即提供与 $A^{\prime}$ 自动正相关的奖励，而奖励制度（或罚款和监禁制度）则必须独立且往往有些随意地估计 $A^{\prime}$（或 $D^{\prime}$）。

## B. The Effectiveness of Public Policy 

The anticipation of conviction and punishment reduces the loss from offenses and thus increases social welfare by discouraging some offenders. What determines the increase in welfare, that is "effectiveness," of public efforts to discourage offenses? The model developed in Section III can be used to answer this question if social welfare is measured by income and if "effectiveness" is defined as a ratio of the maximum feasible increase in income to the increase if all offenses causing net damages were abolished by fiat. The maximum feasible increase is achieved by choosing optimal values of the probability of apprehension and conviction, $p$, and the size of punishments, $f$ (assuming that the coefficient of social loss from punishment, $b$, is given). [^65]
对定罪和惩罚的期待减少了犯罪带来的损失，从而通过威慑一些犯罪者来增加社会福利。那么，是什么决定了公共努力在威慑犯罪方面的福利增加，即“有效性”？如果社会福利以收入来衡量，并且“有效性”被定义为最大可行收入增加与如果通过法令废除所有造成净损害的犯罪所带来的收入增加的比率，那么在第三节中开发的模型可以用来回答这个问题。最大可行的增加是通过选择最佳的逮捕和定罪概率$p$以及惩罚的大小$f$来实现的（假设惩罚造成的社会损失系数$b$是给定的）。[^65]


Effectiveness so defined can vary between zero and unity and depends essentially on two behavioral relations: the costs of apprehension and conviction and the elasticities of response of offenses to changes in $p$ and $f$. The smaller these costs or the greater these elasticities, the smaller the cost of achieving any given reduction in offenses and thus the greater the effectiveness. The elasticities may well differ considerably among different kinds of offenses. For example, crimes of passion, like murder or rape, or crimes of youth, like auto theft, are often said to be less responsive to changes in $p$ and $f$ than are more calculating crimes by adults, like embezzlement, antitrust violation, or bank robbery. The elasticities estimated by Smigel (1965) and Ehrlich (1967) for seven major felonies do differ considerably but are not clearly smaller for murder, rape, auto theft, and assault than for robbery, burglary, and larceny. [^66]
所定义的有效性可以在零和一之间变化，主要取决于两个行为关系： apprehension 和 conviction 的成本，以及对 $p$ 和 $f$ 变化的犯罪反应的弹性。这些成本越小或这些弹性越大，实现任何特定犯罪减少的成本就越小，因此有效性就越高。不同类型的犯罪之间的弹性可能会有很大差异。例如，激情犯罪，如谋杀或强奸，或青少年犯罪，如汽车盗窃，通常被认为对 $p$ 和 $f$ 的变化反应较小，而成年人更具计算性的犯罪，如挪用公款、反垄断违法或银行抢劫，则反应更为敏感。Smigel（1965）和Ehrlich（1967）对七种主要重罪的弹性估计确实存在显著差异，但对于谋杀、强奸、汽车盗窃和攻击的弹性并不明显低于抢劫、入室盗窃和盗窃。 [^66]

Probably effectiveness differs among offenses more because of differences in the costs of apprehension and conviction than in the elasticities of response. An important determinant of these costs, and one that varies greatly, is the time between commission and detection of an offense. [^67] For the earlier an offense is detected, the earlier the police can be brought in and the more likely that the victim is able personally to identify the offender. This suggests that effectiveness is greater for robbery than for a related felony like burglary, or for minimum-wage and fair-employment legislation than for other white-collar legislation like antitrust and publicutility regulation. [^68]
可能不同犯罪之间的有效性差异更多是由于逮捕和定罪成本的差异，而不是反应的弹性差异。这些成本的一个重要决定因素，并且变化很大，是犯罪发生与被发现之间的时间。[^67] 因为越早发现犯罪，警方就能越早介入，受害者也越有可能能够亲自识别犯罪者。这表明，抢劫的有效性比入室盗窃等相关重罪更高，或者比最低工资和公平就业立法更高，而后者则比反垄断和公共事业监管等其他白领立法更高。[^68]

## C. A Theory of Collusion 

The theory developed in this essay can be applied to any effort to preclude certain kinds of behavior, regardless of whether the behavior is "unlawful." As an example, consider efforts by competing firms to collude in order to obtain monopoly profits. Economists lack a satisfactory theory of the determinants of price and output policies by firms in an industry, a theory that could predict under what conditions perfectly competitive, monopolistic, or various intermediate kinds of behavior would emerge. One by-product of our approach to crime and punishment is a theory of collusion that appears to fill a good part of this lacuna. [^69]
本文中提出的理论可以应用于任何旨在预防某些行为的努力，无论该行为是否“违法”。例如，考虑竞争公司之间的勾结，以获取垄断利润。经济学家缺乏一个令人满意的理论来解释行业中公司价格和产出政策的决定因素，这个理论能够预测在什么条件下会出现完全竞争、垄断或各种中间类型的行为。我们对犯罪与惩罚的研究所产生的一个副产品是一个关于勾结的理论，似乎可以填补这一空白的很大一部分。

The gain to firms from colluding is positively related to the elasticity of their marginal cost curves and is inversely related to the elasticity of their collective demand curve. A firm that violates a collusive arrangement by pricing below or producing more than is specified can be said to commit an "offense" against the collusion. The resulting harm to the collusion would depend on the number of violations and on the elasticities of demand and marginal cost curves, since the gain from colluding depends on these elasticities.
企业通过串通获得的收益与其边际成本曲线的弹性呈正相关，与其集体需求曲线的弹性呈负相关。若某企业通过低于规定的价格或超过规定的产量来违反串通协议，可以说该企业对串通行为犯下了“罪行”。对串通造成的伤害将取决于违规的数量以及需求和边际成本曲线的弹性，因为串通所带来的收益依赖于这些弹性。

If violations could be eliminated without cost, the optimal solution would obviously be to eliminate all of them and to engage in pure monopoly pricing. In general, however, as with other kinds of offenses, there are two costs of eliminating violations. There is first of all the cost of discovering violations and of "apprehending" violators. This cost is greater the greater the desired probability of detection and the greater the number of violations. Other things the same, the latter is usually positively related to the number of firms in an industry, which partly explains why economists typically relate monopoly power to concentration. The cost of achieving a given probability of detection also depends on the number of firms, on the number of customers, on the stability of customer buying patterns, and on government policies toward collusive arrangements (see Stigler, 1964).
如果可以在没有成本的情况下消除违规行为，显然最佳的解决方案就是消除所有违规行为并进行纯粹的垄断定价。然而，通常情况下，与其他类型的违法行为一样，消除违规行为有两种成本。首先是发现违规行为和“逮捕”违规者的成本。这个成本随着期望的检测概率和违规行为数量的增加而增加。在其他条件相同的情况下，后者通常与行业内公司的数量呈正相关，这在一定程度上解释了经济学家通常将垄断权力与集中度联系起来的原因。实现给定检测概率的成本还取决于公司的数量、客户的数量、客户购买模式的稳定性以及政府对合谋安排的政策（参见斯蒂格勒，1964年）。

Second, there is the cost to the collusion of punishing violators. The most favorable situation is one in which fines could be levied against violators and collected by the collusion. If fines and other legal recourse are ruled out, methods like predatory price-cutting or violence have to be used, and they hurt the collusion as well as violators.
其次，合谋惩罚违规者的成本也是一个问题。最理想的情况是能够对违规者处以罚款，并由合谋方收取。如果罚款和其他法律手段被排除在外，就必须使用掠夺性降价或暴力等手段，而这些手段不仅会伤害违规者，也会损害合谋本身。

Firms in a collusion are assumed to choose probabilities of detection, punishments to violators, and prices and outputs that minimize their loss from violations, which would at the same time maximize their gain from colluding. Optimal prices and outputs would be closer to the competitive position the more elastic demand curves were, the greater the number of sellers and buyers, the less transferable punishments were, and the more hostile to collusion governments were. Note that misallocation of resources could not be measured simply by the deviation of actual from competitive outputs but would depend also on the cost of enforcing collusions. Note further, and more importantly, that this theory, unlike most theories of pricing, provides for continuous variation, from purely competitive through intermediate situations to purely monopolistic pricing. These situations differ primarily because of differences in the "op'imal" number of violations, which in turn are related to differences in the elasticities, concentrations, legislation, etc., already mentioned.
在合谋中的公司被假设选择检测概率、对违规者的惩罚、以及能够最小化违规损失的价格和产量，同时最大化合谋带来的收益。最优价格和产量会更接近竞争状态，尤其是在需求曲线越富有弹性、卖方和买方数量越多、惩罚越不易转移、以及政府对合谋越敌对的情况下。需要注意的是，资源的错误配置不能仅通过实际产出与竞争产出的偏差来衡量，还需要考虑执行合谋的成本。此外，更重要的是，这一理论与大多数定价理论不同，它提供了从纯竞争到中间情况再到纯垄断定价的连续变化。这些情况的主要区别在于“最优”违规次数的差异，而这又与之前提到的弹性、集中度、立法等差异相关。

These ideas appear to be helpful in understanding the relative success of collusions in illegal industries themselves! Just as firms in legal industries have an incentive to collude to raise prices and profits, so too do firms producing illegal products, such as narcotics, gambling, prostitution, and abortion. The "syndicate" is an example of a presumably highly successful collusion that covers several illegal products. [^70] In a country like the United States that prohibits collusions, those in illegal industries would seem to have an advantage, because force and other illegal methods could be used against violators without the latter having much legal recourse. On the other hand, in countries like prewar Germany that legalized collusions, those in legal industries would have an advantage, because violators could often be legally prosecuted. One would predict, therefore, from this consideration alone, relatively more successful collusions in illegal industries in the United States, and in legal ones in prewar Germany.
这些观点似乎有助于理解非法行业中勾结的相对成功！正如合法行业的公司有动机勾结以提高价格和利润，生产非法产品的公司，如毒品、赌博、卖淫和堕胎等，也同样如此。“ syndicate”（黑帮）就是一个假定非常成功的勾结的例子，涵盖了几种非法产品。在像美国这样的禁止勾结的国家，非法行业的人似乎具有优势，因为可以对违反者使用暴力和其他非法手段，而后者几乎没有法律救济。另一方面，在像战前德国这样的国家，勾结是合法的，合法行业的人则会占据优势，因为违反者往往可以被合法起诉。因此，仅从这一考虑出发，可以预测在美国的非法行业中，勾结相对更成功，而在战前德国的合法行业中则相对更成功。

# VIII. Summary and Concluding Remarks 

This essay uses economic analysis to develop optimal public and private policies to combat illegal behavior. The public's decision variables are its expenditures on police, courts, etc., which help determine the probability $(p)$ that an offense is discovered and the offender apprehended and convicted, the size of the punishment for those convicted $(f)$, and the form of the punishment: imprisonment, probation, fine, etc. Optimal values of these variables can be chosen subject to, among other things, the constraints imposed by three behavioral relations. One shows the damages caused by a given number of illegal actions, called offenses $(O)$, another the cost of achieving a given $p$, and the third the effect of changes in $p$ and $f$ on $O$.
这篇文章利用经济分析来制定最佳的公共和私人政策，以打击非法行为。公众的决策变量包括其在警察、法院等方面的支出，这些支出有助于确定犯罪被发现和罪犯被逮捕及定罪的概率 $(p)$，对被定罪者的惩罚力度 $(f)$，以及惩罚的形式：监禁、缓刑、罚款等。这些变量的最佳值可以在其他条件的约束下进行选择，其中包括三个行为关系所施加的约束。第一个关系显示了特定数量的非法行为（称为犯罪 $(O)$）所造成的损害，第二个关系显示了实现特定 $p$ 的成本，第三个关系则显示了 $p$ 和 $f$ 的变化对 $O$ 的影响。

"Optimal" decisions are interpreted to mean decisions that minimize the social loss in income from offenses. This loss is the sum of damages, costs of apprehension and conviction, and costs of carrying out the punishments imposed, and can be minimized simultaneously with respect to $p, f$, and the form of $f$ unless one or more of these variables is constrained by "outside" considerations. The optimality conditions derived from the minimization have numerous interesting implications that can be illustrated by a few examples.
“最优”决策被解释为能够最小化因犯罪造成的社会收入损失的决策。这种损失是赔偿金、逮捕和定罪的成本以及执行所施加惩罚的成本之和，并且可以在$p$、$f$及$f$的形式方面同时最小化，除非这些变量中的一个或多个受到“外部”因素的限制。从最小化中得出的最优条件具有许多有趣的含义，可以通过几个例子来说明。

If carrying out the punishment were costly, as it is with probation, imprisonment, or parole, the elasticity of response of offenses with respect to a change in $p$ would generally, in equilibrium, have to exceed its response to a change in $f$. This implies, if entry into illegal activities can be explained by the same model of choice that economists use to explain entry into legal activities, that offenders are (at the margin) "risk preferrers." Consequently, illegal activities "would not pay" (at the margin) in the sense that the real income received would be less than what could be received in less risky legal activities. The conclusion that "crime would not pay" is an optimality condition and not an implication about the efficiency of the police or courts; indeed, it holds for any level of efficiency, as long as optimal values of $p$ and $f$ appropriate to each level are chosen.
如果执行惩罚的成本很高，比如缓刑、监禁或假释，那么在均衡状态下，犯罪行为对$p$变化的反应弹性通常必须超过对$f$变化的反应。这意味着，如果进入非法活动可以用经济学家用来解释进入合法活动的同一选择模型来解释，那么犯罪者在边际上是“风险偏好者”。因此，非法活动在边际上“不会有利可图”，因为所获得的实际收入将低于在风险较小的合法活动中可以获得的收入。“犯罪不会有利可图”的结论是一个最优条件，而不是关于警察或法院效率的暗示；实际上，只要选择适合每个效率水平的$p$和$f$的最优值，这一结论在任何效率水平下都成立。

If costs were the same, the optimal values of both $p$ and $f$ would be greater, the greater the damage caused by an offense. Therefore, offenses like murder and rape should be solved more frequently and punished more severely than milder offenses like auto theft and petty larceny. Evidence on actual probabilities and punishments in the United States is strongly consistent with this implication of the optimality analysis.
如果成本相同，那么$p$和$f$的最佳值将随着犯罪造成的损害增加而增加。因此，像谋杀和强奸这样的严重犯罪应该比汽车盗窃和轻微盗窃等轻微犯罪更频繁地被解决并受到更严厉的惩罚。美国关于实际概率和惩罚的证据与这一最佳性分析的含义高度一致。

Fines have several advantages over other punishments: for example, they conserve resources, compensate society as well as punish offenders, and simplify the determination of optimal $p$ 's and $f$ 's. Not surprisingly, fines are the most common punishment and have grown in importance over time. Offenders who cannot pay fines have to be punished in other ways, but the optimality analysis implies that the monetary value to them of these punishments should generally be less than the fines.
罚款相较于其他惩罚有几个优点：例如，它们节约资源，既补偿社会又惩罚违法者，并简化了最佳$p$和$f$的确定。不出所料，罚款是最常见的惩罚方式，并且随着时间的推移其重要性不断增加。无法支付罚款的违法者必须以其他方式受到惩罚，但最优性分析表明，这些惩罚对他们的货币价值通常应低于罚款。

Vengeance, deterrence, safety, rehabilitation, and compensation are perhaps the most important of the many desiderata proposed throughout history. Next to these, minimizing the social loss in income may seem narrow, bland, and even quaint. Unquestionably, the income criterion can be usefully generalized in several directions, and a few have already been suggested in the essay. Yet one should not lose sight of the fact that it is more general and powerful than it may seem and actually includes more dramatic desiderata as special cases. For example, if punishment were by an optimal fine, minimizing the loss in income would be equivalent to compensating "victims" fully and would eliminate the "alarm" that so worried Bentham; or it would be equivalent to deterring all offenses causing great damage if the cost of apprehending, convicting, and punishing these offenders were relatively small. Since the same could also be demonstrated for vengeance or rehabilitation, the moral should be clear: minimizing the loss in income is actually very general and thus is more useful than these catchy and dramatic but inflexible desiderata.
报复、威慑、安全、康复和赔偿或许是历史上提出的众多愿望中最重要的。与这些相比，减少收入的社会损失似乎显得狭隘、平淡，甚至有些古怪。毫无疑问，收入标准可以在多个方向上有益地推广，文中已经提出了一些建议。然而，我们不应忽视这样一个事实：它比看起来更为普遍和强大，实际上还包括了更为戏剧性的愿望作为特例。例如，如果惩罚是通过最优罚款来实施，减少收入损失将等同于对“受害者”进行充分赔偿，并消除本塔姆所担忧的“警报”；或者，如果抓捕、定罪和惩罚这些罪犯的成本相对较小，那么这将等同于威慑所有造成重大损害的犯罪。由于同样的论证也适用于报复或康复，因此道德应该是明确的：减少收入损失实际上是非常普遍的，因此比这些引人注目、戏剧性但不灵活的愿望更为有用。

This essay concentrates almost entirely on determining optimal policies to combat illegal behavior and pays little attention to actual policies. The small amount of evidence on actual policies that I have examined certainly suggests a positive correspondence with optimal policies. For example, it is found for seven major felonies in the United States that more damaging ones are penalized more severely, that the elasticity of response of offenses to changes in $p$ exceeds the response to $f$, and that both are usually less than unity, all as predicted by the optimality analysis. There are, however, some discrepancies too: for example, the actual tradeoff between imprisonment and fines in different statutes is frequently less, rather than the predicted more, favorable to those imprisoned. Although many more studies of actual policies are needed, they are seriously hampered on the empirical side by grave limitations in the quantity and quality of data on offenses, convictions, costs, etc., and on the analytical side by the absence of a reliable theory of political decision-making.
这篇文章几乎完全集中于确定应对非法行为的最佳政策，而对实际政策关注较少。我所研究的关于实际政策的少量证据确实表明与最佳政策之间存在正相关。例如，在美国的七种主要重罪中，发现更严重的罪行受到的惩罚更为严厉，罪行对$p$变化的反应弹性超过对$f$的反应，并且这两者通常都小于单位，这一切都符合最佳性分析的预测。然而，也存在一些差异：例如，不同法规中监禁与罚款之间的实际权衡往往对被监禁者的有利程度较低，而不是预测的更有利。尽管需要更多关于实际政策的研究，但在经验方面，由于对犯罪、定罪、成本等数据的数量和质量存在严重限制，这些研究受到严重阻碍；在分析方面，则缺乏可靠的政治决策理论。

Reasonable men will often differ on the amount of damages or benefits caused by different activities. To some, any wage rates set by competitive labor markets are permissible, while to others, rates below a certain minimum are violations of basic rights; to some, gambling, prostitution, and even abortion should be freely available to anyone willing to pay the market price, while to others, gambling is sinful and abortion is murder. These differences are basic to the development and implementation of public policy but have been excluded from my inquiry. I assume consensus on damages and benefits and simply try to work out rules for an optimal implementation of this consensus.
理性的人们常常会对不同活动所造成的损害或利益的数量产生分歧。对一些人来说，竞争性劳动市场设定的任何工资水平都是可以接受的，而对另一些人来说，低于某一最低标准的工资则是对基本权利的侵犯；对一些人来说，赌博、卖淫甚至堕胎应该对任何愿意支付市场价格的人开放，而对另一些人来说，赌博是罪恶的，堕胎是谋杀。这些差异对于公共政策的制定和实施至关重要，但在我的研究中被排除在外。我假设在损害和利益上达成共识，并仅仅试图制定出一个最佳实施这一共识的规则。

The main contribution of this essay, as I see it, is to demonstrate that optimal policies to combat illegal behavior are part of an optimal allocation of resources. Since economics has been developed to handle resource allocation, an "economic" framework becomes applicable to, and helps enrich, the analysis of illegal behavior. At the same time, certain unique aspects of the latter enrich economic analysis: some punishments, such as imprisonments, are necessarily non-monetary and are a cost to society as well as to offenders; the degree of uncertainty is a decision variable that enters both the revenue and cost functions; etc.
我认为这篇文章的主要贡献在于证明，打击非法行为的最佳政策是资源优化配置的一部分。由于经济学的发展旨在处理资源配置，因此“经济”框架适用于非法行为的分析，并有助于丰富这一分析。同时，后者的一些独特方面也丰富了经济分析：某些惩罚，例如监禁，必然是非货币性的，对社会和违法者都是一种成本；不确定性的程度是一个决策变量，进入收入和成本函数等。

Lest the reader be repelled by the apparent novelty of an "economic" framework for illegal behavior, let him recall that two important contributors to criminology during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, Beccaria and Bentham, explicitly applied an economic calculus. Unfortunately, such an approach has lost favor during the last hundred years, and my efforts can be viewed as a resurrection, modernization, and thereby I hope improvement on these much earlier pioneering studies.
为了避免读者因“经济”框架在非法行为中的明显新颖性而感到排斥，不妨回想一下，十八和十九世纪的两位重要犯罪学贡献者贝卡里亚和边沁，明确地应用了经济计算。不幸的是，这种方法在过去一百年中逐渐失去了青睐，而我的努力可以被视为对这些早期开创性研究的复兴、现代化，因此我希望能有所改进。

# Mathematical Appendix 

This Appendix derives the effects of changes in various parameters on the optimal values of $p$ and $f$. It is assumed throughout that $b>0$ and that equilibrium occurs where

$$
\frac{\partial D}{\partial O}+\frac{\partial C}{\partial O}+\frac{\partial C}{\partial p} \frac{\partial p}{\partial O}=D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}+C_{p} \frac{\partial p}{\partial O}>0
$$

the analysis could easily be extended to cover negative values of $b$ and of this marginal cost term. The conclusion in the text (Sec. II) that $D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}>0$ is relied on here. I take it to be a reasonable first approximation that the elasticities of $O$ with respect to $p$ or $f$ are constant. At several places a sufficient condition for the conclusions reached is that

$$
C_{p O}=C_{O p}=\frac{\partial^{2} C}{\partial p \partial O}=\frac{\partial^{2} C}{\partial O \partial p}
$$

is "small" relative to some other terms. This condition is utilized in the form of a strong assumption that $C_{p O}=0$, although I cannot claim any supporting intuitive or other evidence.

The social loss in income from offenses has been defined as

$$
L=D(O)+C(O, p)+b p f O
$$

If $b$ and $p$ were fixed, the value of $f$ that minimized $L$ would be found from the necessary condition

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial f}=0=\left(D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}\right) \frac{\partial O}{\partial f}+b p f\left(1-E_{f}\right) \frac{\partial O}{\partial f}
$$

or

$$
0=D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}+b p f\left(1-E_{f}\right)
$$

if

$$
\frac{\partial O}{\partial f}=O_{f} \neq 0
$$

where

$$
E_{f}=\frac{-\partial f}{\partial O} \frac{O}{f}
$$

The sufficient condition would be that $\partial^{2} L / \partial f^{2}>0$; using $\partial L / \partial f=0$ and $E_{f}$ is constant, this condition becomes

$$
\frac{\partial^{2} L}{\partial f^{2}}=\left(D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}\right) O_{f}^{2}+b p\left(1-E_{f}\right) O_{f}>0
$$

or

$$
\Delta \equiv D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}+b p\left(1-E_{f}\right) \frac{1}{O_{f}}>0
$$

Since $D^{\prime}+C^{\prime}>0$, and $b$ is not less than zero, equation (A3) implies that $E_{f}>1$. Therefore $\Delta$ would be greater than zero, since we are assuming that $D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}>0$; and $f$, the value of $f$ satisfying (A3), would minimize (locally) the loss $L$.

$$

\frac{d f}{d \alpha}=\frac{-D_{\alpha}^{\prime}\left(1 / O_{f}\right)}{\Delta}
$$

Since $\Delta>0, O_{f}<0$, and by assumption $D_{\alpha}^{\prime}>0$, then

$$
\frac{d f}{d \alpha}=\frac{+}{+}>0
$$

$$
\frac{d f}{d \beta}=\frac{-C_{\beta}^{\prime}\left(1 / O_{f}\right)}{\Delta}=\frac{+}{+}>0
$$

If $b$ is positively related to $\gamma$, then

by (A12), a reduction in $\epsilon_{f}$ would reduce $\hat{f}$.

$$

$$

for it can be shown that $\left(1-E_{p}\right) \Delta>\left(1-E_{f}\right) \Sigma .{ }^{72}$ Note that when $f$ is held constant the optimal value of $p$ is decreased, not increased, by an increase in $\gamma$.

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial s}=\frac{O_{p} p_{O} C_{p s} \Sigma}{+}=\frac{C_{p s} \Sigma}{+}=\frac{+}{+}>0

& \left(D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}\right)\left(E_{f}-E_{p}\right)>-\frac{b p f}{O}\left(1-E_{f}\right)\left[\left(1-E_{p}\right) \frac{f_{O} O}{f}-\left(1-E_{f}\right) \frac{p_{O} O}{p}\right] \\
& \left(D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}\right)\left(E_{f}-E_{p}\right)>-\frac{b p f}{O}\left(1-E_{f}\right)\left[\left(1-E_{p}\right)\left(E_{f}\right)-\left(1-E_{f}\right) E_{p}\right] \\
& \left(D^{\prime \prime}+C^{\prime \prime}\right)\left(E_{f}-E_{p}\right)>-\frac{b p f}{O}\left(1-E_{f}\right)\left(E_{f}-E_{p}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the left-hand side is greater than zero, and the right-hand side is less than zero, the inequality must hold.
